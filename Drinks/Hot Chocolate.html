Hot Chocolate

Ingredients

  • A small amount of chocolate chips
  • Milk
  • Milk or cream
  • Cinnamon
  • Allspice
  • Vanilla
  • Nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Put a small amount of chocolate chips in a mug and cover with milk.
  2. Put in the microwave for 30 seconds.
  3. Dissolve the chocolate in the milk.
  4. In a separate cup, heat up milk/cream.
  5. Whisk up the milk/cream to make it frothy and pour over the chocolate.
  6. Alternatively, add the rest of the milk to the chocolate/milk base and heat up the whole thing again. (Can be done in a pot as well.)
  7. Add spices such as cinnamon, allspice, vanilla, and nutmeg.

Tips

  • Thickening with corn starch slurry: whisk corn starch into a little cold milk until smooth, then stir the slurry into the hot chocolate and heat until thickened. untried
