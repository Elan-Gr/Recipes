Hot Chocolate

Put a small amount of chocolate chips in a mug and cover with milk. Put in microwave for 30 seconds. Dissolve chocolate in milk. In a separate cup, heat up milk/cream. Whisk up milk/cream to make it frothy and pour over chocolate. Alternatively, you can just add the rest of the milk to the chocolate/milk base and heat up the whole thing again. Can do all this in a pot as well. Add spices such as cinnamon, allspice, vanilla, nutmeg.
