Beef Jerkey

Ingredients

  • chuck roast
  • marinade flavorings (of your choice)

Instructions

  1. Cut chuck roast into thin pieces.
  2. Marinate in whatever flavorings you would like.
  3. Lie beef flat on baking sheet (preferably on top of a rack).
  4. Bake at 175 degrees for around 5 hours, flipping meat half way through the bake.
