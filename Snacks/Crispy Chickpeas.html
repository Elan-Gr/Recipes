Crispy Chickpeas

Ingredients

  • 1 15-oz can (425g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tbsp (15ml) olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Rinse and dry canned chickpeas.
  2. Coat in olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Roast at 400 degrees on a baking sheet with parchment paper for about 45 minutes.
  4. Toss the chickpeas halfway through baking.
