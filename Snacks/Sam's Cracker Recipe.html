Sam's Cracker Recipe

Notes

Ground flax and chia seeds act as the binder for these seed-based crackers.

Ingredients

  • 100g sunflower seeds
  • 100g sesame seeds (optional: lightly roast for a tahini-like flavor)
  • 100g ground flax seeds
  • 50g flour (such as spelt or whole wheat)
  • 50g gluten-free flour (e.g., almond flour; can substitute with all GF or all regular flour)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500g hot water

Instructions

  1. In a heat-safe bowl, mix all dry ingredients.
  2. Add hot water and stir until combined.
  3. Cover and let the mixture sit for about 1 hour to thicken.
  4. Preheat oven to 265–280°F (130–140°C).
  5. Roll out the dough between two sheets of parchment paper as thinly and evenly as possible.
  6. Bake for 30–45 minutes, or until the crackers are completely dry.
  7. Use a knife to score break lines in the dough partway through baking if desired.
  8. Allow to cool fully, then break into pieces.
