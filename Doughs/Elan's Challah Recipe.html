Elan's Challah Recipe

Ingredients

  • 600g all purpose or bread flour (bread flour preferred)
  • 7g (1 packet) instant yeast
  • 375g water
  • 1 egg (plus extra for egg wash)
  • 68g (5 tbsp) olive oil
  • 6g (1 tsp) salt
  • 42g (4 tbsp) sugar

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients in a large bowl until fully combined.
  2. Let the dough rest for 2 hours and perform 3 sets of bowl folds:
    • First fold after 30 minutes
    • Second fold after 60 minutes
    • Third fold after 90 minutes
  3. At this point, you can put the bread in the fridge for a day or so if you need to make the dough ahead of time. Take it out of the fridge at least 45 minutes before shaping
  4. Braid the dough into desired shape. Dough may be easier to braid if it slightly cold. First, divide the dough in three portions roll each portion into a ball. Let balls of dough rest for 15 minutes at room temperature before rolling into three strands and braiding. It is okay if the strands look a little bit rough. Work on a well-floured work surface.
  5. Proof Challah for 20 minutes at room temperature.
  6. Brush with egg wash.
  7. Bake at 390°F (200°C) for 30–35 minutes. Cover during baking in bread oven / roasting pan for best results.

Yield: 2 medium-sized challahs or 1 large challah
