# Rustic Yeast Bread Recipe

Makes one loaf.

## Ingredients

- 500 grams of flour (white and a little spelt or rye; use 50–80% white, and the rest a dark flour)
- 8 grams of salt
- 333 grams of warm water
- 0.5 teaspoon instant yeast (try 1 teaspoon if that isn't enough)
- Optional: Add 100 grams active or non-active sourdough starter for sourdough taste

## Instructions

1. Mix ingredients with a spoon until mixed.
2. Add whatever other seeds you want to add.
3. Let dough sit for half an hour at room temperature.
4. Stretch/fold/knead the bread (2–3 minutes).
5. Cover and let sit for another few hours (between 3 and 5 hours).
   - Every 30 min to an hour, wet hands and gently fold the dough for 2–3 minutes. This gently kneads the dough to help develop the gluten, and incorporates air into the dough to make it less dense.
   - Do this up to about 3 times in 5 hours.
6. Shape dough by folding in the edges, and put in a loaf pan or shaping vessel to let it sit to proof. Aim for 1.5 to 2 hours. But you can keep it in the fridge overnight to 24 hours instead of the three hours at room temperature for stronger sourdough taste.
7. Score the bread about 1/4 to 1/2 inches thick.
8. Put shaped dough into preheated Dutch oven.
9. Bake for 45 minutes at 525°F (or 45 minutes at 400°F if using an uncovered loaf pan, or 18–20 minutes at 450°F for rolls). Place an ice cube or two in the enclosed baking dish, but make sure it is somewhere where when it melts, the bread will not be sitting in a pool of water.
   - You can take the cover off for the last 15 minutes of baking for a darker crust.
