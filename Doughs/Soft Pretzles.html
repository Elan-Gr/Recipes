Soft Pretzles

Ingredients

For the pretzels

  • 1 cup (240 grams) warm milk
  • 1 packet (7 grams) instant yeast
  • 1 tablespoon (12.5 grams) sugar
  • 2.5 cups (312 grams) all purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter (room temperature)

For dunking

  • 3 cups boiling water
  • 1/3 cup baking soda

For topping

  • Melted butter
  • Coarse salt

Instructions

  1. Mix dough ingredients in a large bowl.
  2. Knead dough for 5 minutes.
  3. Roll into ball and let proof for 60–90 minutes.
  4. Divide dough into 6 equal pieces (or more for mini pretzels) and shape into pieces.
  5. Mix baking soda into boiling water.
  6. Dip/dunk each pretzel into boiling baking soda mixture and place on baking sheet.
  7. Brush with egg wash for a soft crust.
  8. Bake at 450°F for 8–10 minutes.
  9. Dip/brush with melted butter and sprinkle with salt.
