Bagels

Ingredients

  • 520 g bread flour
  • 7 g (1 packet) instant yeast
  • 360 g water
  • 11 g salt
  • 12 g sugar

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients in a large bowl until fully combined.
  2. Let the dough rest for 2 hours and perform 3 sets of bowl folds:
  • First fold after 30 minutes
  • Second fold after 60 minutes
  • Third fold after 90 minutes
  1. At this point, you can put the bread in the fridge for a day or so if you need to make the dough ahead of time. Take it out of the fridge at least 45 minutes before shaping.
  2. Shape dough into balls.
  3. Proof for 10 minutes.
  4. Shape bagels.
  5. Boil in water bath with 2 quarts of water and 85 grams honey or barley malt syrup for 60 seconds on each side.
  6. Bake at 425°F for 25 minutes.

Yield: 8 bagels
