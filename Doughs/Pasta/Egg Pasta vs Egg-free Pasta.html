Egg Pasta vs Egg-free Pasta

Egg-Based Pasta

  • Ingredients: Flour (often "00") + Eggs
  • Texture: Rich, tender, slightly yellow
  • Common in: Fresh pasta (Northern Italy)

Shapes:

  • Tagliatelle
  • Fettuccine
  • Pappardelle
  • Lasagna sheets (fresh)
  • Tortellini
  • Ravioli
  • Agnolotti
  • Malfatti
  • Garganelli

Egg-Free Pasta

  • Ingredients: Semolina flour + Water
  • Texture: Firmer, more al dente
  • Common in: Dried pasta (Southern Italy)

Shapes:

  • Spaghetti
  • Penne
  • Rigatoni
  • Macaroni
  • Fusilli
  • Orecchiette
  • Ziti
  • Bucatini
  • Linguine (usually egg-free when dried)
  • Trofie
  • Conchiglie (shells)

Summary

  • Fresh pasta: Usually egg-based
  • Dried pasta: Typically egg-free
  • Stuffed pasta: Almost always contains egg
