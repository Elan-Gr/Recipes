Ciabatta or Baguette

Ingredients

  • 400 grams of all-purpose or bread flour (use up to half whole grain)
  • 1.5 grams (1/4 teaspoon) instant yeast
  • 6 grams (1 teaspoon) salt
  • 1 1/2 cups hot water (354 ml) — about 125–130°F (can increase water up to about 100% hydration, or reduce for denser dough to make bagels)

Instructions (Quick Ferment)

  1. Combine the flour, yeast, and salt in a large bowl. Stir in the hot water until the ingredients are well combined.
  2. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let the dough stand at room temperature for about 3 hours or until the dough has doubled in size. It should become puffy and dotted with bubbles. Can do some stretch and folds during that time, but not necessary.
  3. At this point, you can transfer bowl to fridge for 24–72 hours for better flavor. Remove from fridge at least an hour before baking.
  4. Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C) and grease a bread pan or line Dutch oven.
  5. Transfer the dough to a well-floured surface and sprinkle it with a little flour. Using a scraper, fold the dough over 10–12 times and shape it into a rough ball. (If the surface is not well-floured, you won't incorporate enough flour for the dough to hold its shape.)
  6. Place the shaped dough into the greased bread pan and cover it with a towel. Let it stand on the countertop for about 35 minutes.
  7. Once the oven reaches 450°F (232°C), carefully place the bread pan in the preheated oven. Bake for approximately 35–40 minutes or until the bread is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped.
  8. After baking, remove the bread from the pan and let it cool on a wire rack for at least 15 minutes before slicing.
