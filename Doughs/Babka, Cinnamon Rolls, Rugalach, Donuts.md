# Babka / Cinnamon Roll / Rugalach

## To Make the Dough

1. Make challah dough recipe.
2. Fill and shape dough using instructions below.
3. Let shaped dough proof for an additional 20 minutes.
4. Coat in egg wash if you want golden top.
5. Bake at 300°F for around 30–35 minutes uncovered.
6. Immediately after baking, pour a sugar syrup over the top, if making babka or rugalach.

## To Make the Filling

### Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (99 grams) granulated sugar (or brown sugar for more gooey filling)
- 1/3 cup (28 grams) unsweetened cocoa powder or 5 teaspoons of cinnamon
- 4 tablespoons (57 grams) melted butter

### Instructions

1. Combine the sugar, cocoa/cinnamon. Stir in the melted butter. The mixture will look grainy and slick; that's OK.

**Note:** This is probably the smallest amount of filling that I would use for one batch of dough. Feel free to double it.

**Note:** When making this recipe, especially when making cinnamon rolls, you can pour some warm cream over the unbaked cinnamon rolls to make the filling more gooey.

**Note:** This filling will essentially form a chocolatey/cinnamonny crust along the inside of the filling. If you are looking for a thicker filling, consider using a chocolate spread instead.

## Shaping a Classic Babka

1. Roll dough into a thin sheet.
2. Spread a layer of chocolate spread on half of the dough.
3. Fold sheet and seal the edges.
4. Roll out the folded dough and spread another layer of chocolate across the surface.
5. Sprinkle with chocolate chips or chopped chocolate.
6. Roll up the dough into a log.
7. Cut the log in half the long way.
8. Twist the halves of the log with the cut end facing up and place into baking sheet.

## Donuts

This dough also makes good donuts. Just know that if you are shallow frying it, the outside will cook very quickly in hot oil, so make small donuts like beignets so they will be cooked all the way through.
