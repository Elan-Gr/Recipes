Flour Tortillas

Ingredients

  • 300 grams (1.5 cups) all purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 50 grams oil
  • 200 grams (7/8 cup) hot water

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, baking powder, salt.
  2. Add oil and stir until oil coats most of flour.
  3. Pour in hot water and stir briskly until combined.
  4. Form dough into ball and cover.
  5. Let sit for 15–30 minutes.
  6. Roll dough thinly with rolling pin on a non-floured surface into eight 8-inch circles.
  7. Cook on ungreased pan over medium high heat until you see small brown bubbles and repeat on other side.

Yield: 8 tortillas
