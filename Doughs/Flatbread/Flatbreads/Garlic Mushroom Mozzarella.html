Flatbread: Garlic, Mushroom, and Mozzarella

Makes two 10-inch flatbreads. Garlic oil base, browned mushrooms, and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 300g (2 1/2 cups plus 1 tbsp) '00' pizza flour
  • 222g (1 scant cup) lukewarm water
  • 1 1/2 tsp (5g) instant yeast
  • 15g (1 generous tbsp) olive oil
  • 6g (1 tsp) table salt
  • Semolina flour or cornmeal, for dusting (about 20g)
  • 200g (about 2 1/2 cups) mushrooms, sliced
  • 250g (9 oz) low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
  • 3 tbsp (45g) olive oil, divided, plus more for grilling
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried thyme

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: combine the flour, water, yeast, olive oil, and salt in a large bowl. Mix until a shaggy, sticky dough forms with no dry patches, under 1 minute. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Bowl-fold: with a wet hand, grab a section of dough, lift, and press to the middle. Turn the bowl 90 degrees and repeat, 8 to 12 folds total. Cover and rest at room temperature 30 minutes to 3 hours.
  3. Turn out onto a floured surface, deflate gently, and divide in half (about 270g each). Shape into balls, cover, and rest 20 minutes to 1 hour.
  4. Stretch each ball on a semolina-dusted surface into a 10-inch round.
  5. Sauté the mushrooms in 1 tbsp of the olive oil over high heat 5 minutes until browned; season with half the salt.
  6. Mix the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil with the garlic and thyme.
  7. Stretch the dough as directed. Brush each round with the garlic oil, top with mushrooms and mozzarella, and sprinkle with the remaining salt.
  8. Grill: preheat the grill to 350-400°F (175-200°C). Brush one side of the dough with olive oil and grill oil-side down 3 to 4 minutes until golden. (Oven alternative: bake on a preheated steel or stone at 450°F for 5 to 7 minutes.)
  9. Slice and serve.
