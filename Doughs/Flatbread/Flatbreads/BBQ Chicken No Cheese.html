Flatbread: BBQ Chicken (No Cheese)

Makes two 10-inch flatbreads. Smoky BBQ chicken with red onion and cilantro — no cheese, keeping it kosher with the meat.

Ingredients

  • 300g (2 1/2 cups plus 1 tbsp) '00' pizza flour
  • 222g (1 scant cup) lukewarm water
  • 1 1/2 tsp (5g) instant yeast
  • 15g (1 generous tbsp) olive oil
  • 6g (1 tsp) table salt
  • Semolina flour or cornmeal, for dusting (about 20g)
  • 300g (about 2 cups) cooked shredded chicken
  • 120g (1/2 cup) BBQ sauce
  • 75g (1/2 medium) red onion, thinly sliced
  • 10g (1/4 cup) fresh cilantro leaves
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil, plus more for grilling

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: combine the flour, water, yeast, olive oil, and salt in a large bowl. Mix until a shaggy, sticky dough forms with no dry patches, under 1 minute. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Bowl-fold: with a wet hand, grab a section of dough, lift, and press to the middle. Turn the bowl 90 degrees and repeat, 8 to 12 folds total. Cover and rest at room temperature 30 minutes to 3 hours.
  3. Turn out onto a floured surface, deflate gently, and divide in half (about 270g each). Shape into balls, cover, and rest 20 minutes to 1 hour.
  4. Stretch each ball on a semolina-dusted surface into a 10-inch round.
  5. Toss the shredded chicken with the BBQ sauce.
  6. Stretch the dough as directed. Spread half the chicken mixture on each round, leaving a 1/2-inch border. Scatter the red onion over the top.
  7. Grill: preheat the grill to 350-400°F (175-200°C). Brush one side of the dough with olive oil and grill oil-side down 3 to 4 minutes until golden. (Oven alternative: bake on a preheated steel or stone at 450°F for 5 to 7 minutes.)
  8. Top with cilantro, slice, and serve.
