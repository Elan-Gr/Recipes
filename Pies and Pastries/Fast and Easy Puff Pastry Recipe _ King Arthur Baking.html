Fast and Easy Puff Pastry Recipe - King Arthur Baking

Source: King Arthur Baking — <https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/fast-and-easy-puff-pastry-recipe>

Wonderfully flaky, buttery, tender pastries, turnovers, cheese straws, elephant ears (palmiers), shells for savory fillings, and more with a minimum of effort. Since this pastry uses only a few ingredients, quality matters.

Prep: 30 mins · Total: 1 hr · Yield: 1 generous pound puff pastry, enough to roll into a 12" x 24" rectangle

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (240g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon table salt*
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 16 tablespoons (227g) unsalted butter, cold, cut in pats*
  • 1/2 cup (113g) sour cream

\* If you use salted butter, reduce the salt to 1/4 teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the flour, salt, and baking powder.
  2. Add the butter, working it in to make a coarse/crumbly mixture. Leave most of the butter in large, pea-sized pieces.
  3. Stir in the sour cream; the dough won't be cohesive. Turn it out onto a floured work surface and bring it together with a few quick kneads.
  4. Pat the dough into a rough log and roll into an 8" x 10" rectangle.
  5. Dust both sides of the dough with flour and, starting with a shorter end, fold it in three like a business letter.
  6. Flip the dough over, give it a 90° turn on the work surface, and roll it into an 8" x 10" rectangle. Fold it in three again.
  7. Chill the dough for at least 30 minutes before using. To make pastry, roll into desired size.
  8. Freeze dough for prolonged storage, up to 2 months. To use, thaw in the refrigerator overnight.

Tips from our Bakers

  • Puff pastry sausage rolls: Divide the puff pastry into four equal pieces and roll each into a 4" x 12" rectangle. Divide 1 pound of uncooked sausage meat into four equal pieces; form each into a 3/4"-diameter rope. Place the rope down the center length of a rectangle. Wet one of the longer edges with water and roll the other edge over the filling to enclose it. Place the finished roll on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Repeat with the remaining ingredients. Place the baking sheet in the freezer for 30 to 40 minutes to firm up. Cut the semi-frozen rolls at 1" intervals — you should get 12 pieces from each roll. Place the individual rolls, seam-side down, on lightly greased or parchment-lined baking sheets. Bake in a preheated 400°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes, until golden brown. Cool slightly before serving. Yield: 48 small sausage rolls.
