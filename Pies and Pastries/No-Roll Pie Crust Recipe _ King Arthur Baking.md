# No-Roll Pie Crust

Source: King Arthur Baking — <https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/no-roll-pie-crust-recipe>

Many folks swear by the convenience and quality of a no-roll pie crust made with vegetable oil. This crisp, tasty crust is easy to press into a pan and has the added bonus of being vegan! For a two-crust pie, see "baker's tips," below.

**Prep:** 7 mins · **Total:** 7 mins · **Yield:** single 9" crust

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (240g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- Heaping 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 3/8 teaspoon baking powder
- 7 tablespoons (85g) canola oil, vegetable oil, olive oil, or peanut oil, your choice
- 1/4 cup (57g) water, cold

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the flour, salt, sugar, and baking powder.
2. Whisk together the oil and water, then pour over the dry ingredients.
3. Stir with a fork until the dough is evenly moistened.
4. Pat the dough across the bottom of the pie pan and up the sides. A flat-bottomed measuring cup or glass helps smooth the bottom.
5. Crimp the edge, or flatten with the tines of a fork.
6. Fill the crust as desired, and bake.

## Tips from our Bakers

- **Melted butter instead of oil?** Go right ahead — it'll have lovely buttery flavor. The crust will no longer be vegan.
- **Two-crust pie:** Increase the ingredients to: 3 cups (360g) flour, 1 teaspoon salt, 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar, 1/2 teaspoon baking powder, 2/3 cup (132g) vegetable oil, 6 tablespoons (85g) water. Press 2/3 of the dough into the pie pan, then add 1 teaspoon cinnamon and 2 tablespoons sugar to the remainder. After filling the bottom crust, break the topping into small pieces and spread evenly over the filling. The topping will crisp up into something between streusel and a typical top crust.
