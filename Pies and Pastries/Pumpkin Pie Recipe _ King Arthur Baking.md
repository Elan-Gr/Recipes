# Pumpkin Pie

Source: King Arthur Baking — <https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/pumpkin-pie-recipe>

A rich, spicy pumpkin pie that slices well and has a bright flavor. Two hints for a better result: mix the filling together a day in advance to let the spices blend, and remove the pie from the oven while the center is still wobbly — overcooking will cause the filling to crack.

**Prep:** 30 mins · **Bake:** 45 to 50 mins · **Total:** 1 hr 15 mins · **Yield:** one 9" pie

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup (106g) light brown sugar or dark brown sugar, packed
- 1 tablespoon King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon ginger*
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon*
- 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg*
- 1/8 teaspoon cloves*
- 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, optional
- 3 large eggs, beaten
- 2 scant cups (425g) or one 15-ounce can pumpkin purée
- 1 1/4 cups (283g) light cream or evaporated milk

\* Substitute a generous 2 1/2 teaspoons Pumpkin Pie Spice for the spices listed, if desired.

### Crust

Use your favorite single pie crust recipe, or King Arthur's Classic Single Pie Crust. Try replacing 1/4 cup (30g) of the all-purpose flour in your pastry with 1/4 cup (35g) cornmeal for additional texture.

## Instructions

1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the sugars, flour, salt, and spices.
2. In a large measuring cup, beat together the eggs, pumpkin, and cream or evaporated milk. Whisk into the dry ingredients. For best flavor, cover and refrigerate the filling overnight before baking (lets the spices' flavor blend).
3. Lightly grease a 9" pie pan that's at least 1 1/2" deep. Roll the pie dough out to a 13" circle and transfer to the pan. Crimp the edges above the rim — this gives extra headroom to hold the filling when it expands in the oven. Refrigerate the crust while the oven preheats to 400°F.
4. When the oven is hot, place the pie pan on a baking sheet to catch drips. Pour the filling into the unbaked pie shell.
5. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until the filling is set 2" in from the edge. The center should still be wobbly. Remove from the oven and cool on a rack; the center finishes cooking through as the pie sits. Store leftover pumpkin pie, covered, in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

## Tips from our Bakers

- **Why remove early:** Pumpkin pie filling is basically a custard; the eggs keep cooking as heat from the edge moves toward the center. Leaving it in the oven too long overcooks the eggs, tightening the proteins and causing the pie to crack in the center.
- **Masala Pumpkin Pie:** Change the spice mixture: decrease cinnamon to 1/2 teaspoon, increase ground cloves to 1/4 teaspoon and black pepper to 1/2 teaspoon; add 1/2 teaspoon each ground coriander and turmeric, and 1/4 teaspoon each ground cardamom and cumin.
- This recipe makes enough filling to generously fill a 9" pan that's at least 1 1/2" deep. If your pan isn't quite that big, bake leftover filling in custard cups; it takes 25 to 30 minutes to cook.
- **Decoration:** To dress up the pie, cut out and bake King Arthur's Light Spice Holiday Cookies according to the recipe; cool and place them around the perimeter of the cooled pie.
