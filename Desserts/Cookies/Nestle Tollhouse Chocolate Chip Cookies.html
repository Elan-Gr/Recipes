Nestle Tollhouse Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups (280 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup (230 grams) butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup (150 grams) white sugar
  • 3/4 cup (160 grams) packed brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups (250 grams) semisweet chocolate chips (such as Nestlé Toll House)
  • 1 cup (150 grams) chopped nuts

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Combine flour, baking soda and salt in small bowl.
  3. Beat butter, white sugar, brown sugar and vanilla in a large mixing bowl until creamy. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Gradually beat in flour mixture. Stir in chocolate morsels and nuts.
  4. Drop by rounded tablespoon onto ungreased baking sheets.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown, 9 to 11 minutes, switching racks halfway through.
  6. Cool on the baking sheets for 2 minutes, then transfer to wire racks to cool completely.

Makes 12 large cookies or more smaller ones.

If baking cookie dough balls from frozen, add an extra 2 or 3 minutes to the time.
