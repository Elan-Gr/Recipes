Candy Bar Shortbread Bars

Ingredients

  • 2 sticks butter, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • Chopped Halloween candy (caramel and peanut butter–caramel bars), a generous amount
  • 1 (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk

Instructions

  1. Cream the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour to make the shortbread base.
  2. Mix in the chopped candy.
  3. Swirl in the condensed milk lightly — don't mix it all the way in.
  4. Press into a pan and bake at 325°F for 7 minutes, then lower the oven to 300°F and bake until browned.

Pan size and candy amount still to be added.
