Soft Cranberry Sugar Cookies

Ingredients

  • 283 grams sweetened dried cranberries
  • 300 grams (1.5 cups) white sugar
  • 184 grams (1 cup) vegetable shortening
  • 2 eggs
  • 420 grams (3.5 cups) all purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Directions

  1. Combine sugar, shortening, eggs, vanilla.
  2. Stir in flour and baking soda.
  3. Stir in dried cranberries.
  4. Shape into balls.
  5. Bake for 10–15 minutes at 350°F.

Makes around 24 cookies.
