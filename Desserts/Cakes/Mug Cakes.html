Mug Cakes

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 14 grams chopped chocolate
  • 1/8 cup (25 grams) sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tablespoon (7 grams) cocoa powder
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 cup (18 grams) flour
  • ¼ teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Melt butter and chocolate in 12 oz mug in microwave
  2. Whisk wet ingredients, then dry ingredients into mug
  3. Microwave for 45 seconds
  4. Let cake sit for 2 minutes before serving
