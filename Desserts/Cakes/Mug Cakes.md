# Mug Cake

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons butter
- 14 grams chopped chocolate
- 1/8 cup (25 grams) sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tablespoon (7 grams) cocoa powder
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 cup (18 grams) flour
- ¼ teaspoon baking powder

## Directions

- Melt butter and chocolate in 12 oz mug in microwave
- Whisk wet ingredients, then dry ingredients into mug
- Microwave for 45 seconds
- Let cake sit for 2 minutes before serving
