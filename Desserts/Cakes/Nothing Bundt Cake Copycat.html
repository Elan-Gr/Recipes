Nothing Bundt Cake Copycat

Makes 1 bundt pan or 1.5–2 round pans.

White Cake

Ingredients

  • 170 grams vegetable oil
  • 198 g (1 cup) granulated sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 140 g (2 cups) all purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon table salt
  • 100 g sour cream
  • Extracts and flavorings

Instructions

  1. Whisk together wet ingredients
  2. Add dry ingredients and stir
  3. Pour into baking pan
  4. Bake for 25–30 minutes at 325 degrees
  5. As soon as you can take it out of the pan, wrap it in saran wrap, and put in freezer for at least 12–24 hours
  6. Thaw, frost, and eat

Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 170 grams vegetable oil
  • 198 g (1 cup) granulated sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 105 g all purpose flour

  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon table salt

Instructions

  4. Bake for 25–30 minutes at 325 degrees
  5. As soon as you can take it out of the pan, wrap it in saran wrap, and put in freezer for at least 12–24 hours
