Fruit Cake Recipe

A simple, moist fruit cake with a tender-but-dense crumb, warm spices, and plenty of room to make it your own. Use whatever combination of dried, candied, or preserved fruit you like — the important thing is keeping the total amount of fruit about the same.

  • Makes: 1 large 8-inch cake
  • Oven: 285°F (140°C) fan / 310°F (155°C) conventional
  • Bake time: about 75–90 minutes
  • Best texture: after resting 1–2 days

Ingredients

Fruit mixture

  • 350 g (about 2 cups) mixed dried/candied fruit, chopped as needed
  • 100 g (about ½ cup) dates or another soft dried fruit, chopped
  • 165 g (⅔ cup) fruit juice
  • 150 g (¾ cup packed) brown sugar
  • 35 g (2 tbsp) molasses
  • 1–2 tsp citrus zest, optional
  • 60 g (¼ cup) neutral oil or butter
  • ¼ tsp salt, if using unsalted butter

Cake batter

  • 210 g (1¾ cups) all-purpose flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ½–1 tsp allspice, to taste
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 3 large eggs, room temperature
  • 100 g (1 cup) chopped nuts, optional

Optional finishing syrup

  • 50 g (3 tbsp) fruit juice
  • 20–25 g (1½–2 tbsp) granulated sugar

Choosing Your Fruit

This is meant to be customizable. For the 450 g total fruit, aim for a mixture with different textures:

  • Soft/chewy fruit: about 150–250 g
  • Smaller dried fruit: about 100–200 g
  • Candied or preserved fruit: about 50–150 g
  • Dates or another particularly soft fruit: up to about 100 g

You don't need all of these categories. Just keep the total around 450 g.

A few guidelines:

  • Chop large pieces so they are roughly the size of the smaller dried fruit.
  • Very dry, firm fruit benefits from being softened before baking.
  • Very soft fruit can be stirred in later so it doesn't completely disappear into the cake.
  • If your fruit is especially sweet, you can reduce the brown sugar slightly.
  • If your fruit is very dry, use the full amount of juice.
  • If using particularly juicy or syrup-packed fruit, drain it well before weighing.

Instructions

1. Prepare the pan

Grease an 8-inch × 3-inch deep cake pan and line the bottom with parchment paper.

Preheat the oven to 285°F (140°C) with a fan, or 310°F (155°C) without a fan.

2. Soften the fruit

Add the fruit, dates, brown sugar, juice, molasses, citrus zest, and 60 g neutral oil or dairy-free butter to a large saucepan.

Heat over medium heat until the mixture reaches a gentle simmer.

Simmer for 3–5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Microwave shortcut: If you don't want to use the stovetop, place the fruit, dates, juice, brown sugar, molasses, and citrus zest in a large microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 30–60 second intervals, stirring between each, until the mixture is hot and the fruit has softened. Stir in the oil or oil butter afterward.

Remove from the heat and allow the mixture to cool to room temperature.

Tip: The microwave method is especially useful if your fruit is very dry. Let the fruit sit in the hot juice mixture for 5–10 minutes before continuing so it has more time to absorb the liquid.

3. Mix the dry ingredients

Whisk together the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, allspice, and salt.

4. Add the eggs

Whisk the eggs together, then stir them into the cooled fruit mixture until evenly incorporated.

5. Add the nuts

Fold in the chopped nuts, if using.

6. Add the flour

Add the dry ingredients to the fruit mixture. Fold gently until just combined. The batter will be very thick.

7. Bake

Transfer the batter to the prepared pan and spread evenly.

Bake for approximately 75–90 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

If the top becomes too dark before the center is cooked, loosely cover it with foil.

8. Cool

Cool in the pan for about 30 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack and cool completely.

9. Optional syrup

Heat the fruit juice and sugar together just until the sugar dissolves.

While the cake is still warm, brush the syrup over the top and sides.

10. Rest

Wrap the cooled cake tightly and store in an airtight container.

For the best texture, let it rest for 24–48 hours before cutting.

Easy Ways to Customize It

  • Less sweet: Reduce the brown sugar to 125–135 g.
  • More spice: Increase the cinnamon or allspice, or add a little cardamom or cloves.
  • More citrus: Add extra zest and use a complementary citrus juice.
  • Nut-free: Simply leave out the nuts.
  • Darker and richer: Increase the molasses to 45–50 g.
  • Lighter: Use less molasses and a lighter-colored juice.
  • Extra fruity: Use the full 450 g fruit mixture, but avoid substantially increasing the amount without adjusting the rest of the recipe.

The Basic Formula

450 g fruit + 165 g juice + 150 g brown sugar + 35 g molasses + 260 g flour + 3 eggs + 60 g neutral oil or dairy-free butter + spices

Then add: 100 g nuts (optional)

This gives you a dependable base while leaving the fruit, spices, nuts, and juice open to customization.
