# Chocolate Ganache

Mix around one-part chocolate and one-part hot cream (226 g chocolate for 240 g cream). Stir until smooth.

Once ganache has cooled, you can whip it up and use it as frosting. Otherwise, you can pour slightly warm ganache over cake.
