# Steamed Eggs Cook Time

(Transcribed from image: "STEAMED EGGS COOK TIME" chart showing halved steamed eggs at different times)

| Steam time | Doneness (yolk) |
|---|---|
| 6 min | Very runny, liquid yolk |
| 7 min | Slightly set, still runny |
| 8 min | Mostly set, soft yolk |
| 9 min | Firm yolk |
