Poultry Pasteurization Times

Poultry is safe to eat when held at a given internal temperature for the corresponding time below (7-log10 reduction of Salmonella, per USDA FSIS time-temperature tables). This is why chicken breast pulled at 150–155°F is safe once it rests — it doesn't have to hit 165°F.

Key numbers

  • 145°F — 9.2 minutes
  • 150°F — 2.8 minutes
  • 155°F — 0.8 minutes (about 50 seconds)
  • 160°F — 0.2 minutes (about 12 seconds)
  • 165°F — instant
