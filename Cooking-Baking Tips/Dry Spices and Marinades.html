Dry Spices and Marinades

Dry spices will burn at temperatures above around 375 degrees, so if you need to cook above 375 degrees, consider using a marinade.

Applies to any protein cooked above 375°F.

Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Flavorings
  • Salt
  • Acid (optional)

Instructions

  1. Combine the oil, flavorings, salt, and acid (if using) to make the marinade.
  2. Let the protein marinate for at least a couple of hours, or overnight.
