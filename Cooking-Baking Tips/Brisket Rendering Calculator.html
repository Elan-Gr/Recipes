How it works

  • Cook Rendering = sum over the cook rows of (hours × that row's rate).
  • Hold Rendering = the same sum over the hold rows.
  • Estimated Final Rendering = Cook Rendering + Hold Rendering — simple addition. The displayed percentage is not capped at 100% (the progress bar stops visually at 100).
  • Other cuts: this calculator is calibrated for brisket. The same collagen-rendering logic applies to other collagen-rich beef cuts — chuck roast is the closest analogue, and beef ribs are also high in collagen — but the percentages are not calibrated for them, so use it as a rough guide and judge doneness by probe tenderness.
  • Exact Temp checked: the rate at the zone's starting temperature. Unchecked: the weighted average rate for the whole zone, computed by averaging the exact interpolated rate at each whole degree in the zone.

Changelog

  • August 30, 2026: Temperature entries now represent weighted rendering rates across the temperature zones (i.e. 150 is now the weighted rendering rate from 150 to 159°F) rather than a single exact temperature, giving a more realistic rendering estimate. An "Exact Temp" option has also been added for controlled cooks or holds (for example, if you are holding exactly at 150°F then check the "Exact Temp" box for that field).
