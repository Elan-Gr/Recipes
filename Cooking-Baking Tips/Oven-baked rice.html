Oven-baked rice

Recipe by Faigy Murray — <https://mykitchenmystudio.com/perfect-every-time-oven-baked-rice/>

The best rice ever — no rice cooker needed!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice (any kind)
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tsp oil (optional)
  • Optional add-ins of your choice (sautéed veggies, grilled sausage, fried pastrami, etc.)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Place rice and water in a 9x13 and cover tightly.
  3. Bake for 40 minutes.
  4. Remove from oven. If the rice is fully cooked and there is only a little bit of liquid left, fluff with a fork and cover the pan again. The steaming process will allow it to absorb remaining liquid.
  5. If the rice is still crunchy and there's a lot of liquid at the bottom of the pan, return to the oven for another 5–10 minutes and check again. Repeat until rice is fully cooked.
  6. If serving the rice plain, toss with 2 teaspoons oil before serving. If adding anything (veggies, sausage, etc.), add and mix before serving.

Notes

Freezes well. (No, that's not a mistake. Yes, you can freeze rice!)
