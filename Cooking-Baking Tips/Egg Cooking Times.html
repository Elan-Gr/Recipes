Egg Cooking Times

(Transcribed from image: "STEAMED EGGS COOK TIME" chart showing halved steamed eggs at different times)

Ingredients

  • Eggs

Instructions

Steam the eggs for the time matching the desired doneness:

Steam timeDoneness (yolk)
6 minVery runny, liquid yolk
7 minSlightly set, still runny
8 minMostly set, soft yolk
9 minFirm yolk
