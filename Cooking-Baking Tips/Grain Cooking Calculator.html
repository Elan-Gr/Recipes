Notes

  • One serving = 1/4 cup of dry grain. The calculator shows how many grams that is for the selected grain.
  • 1 cup of water = 237 ml = 237 g.
  • Firm and Soft shift the Standard ratio by 1/4 cup of water per cup of grain — except the KitchenAid rice types, where Firm and Soft are the ends of the manual's published range and Standard is the midpoint.
  • Rinse rice before cooking. Rest grains covered, off the heat, about 10 minutes before fluffing.
  • Farro is often boiled like pasta in plenty of water and drained instead; the ratios above are for the covered-pot absorption method.
  • Fine bulgur needs less water than medium or coarse; the table is for medium/coarse. Soak it rather than boiling it.
  • Cup weights vary by brand and grind, especially for bulgur and buckwheat — weigh the grain when it matters.
  • Ratios are starting points. Age, variety, and how much the grain was rinsed all shift absorption; taste near the end and adjust.
