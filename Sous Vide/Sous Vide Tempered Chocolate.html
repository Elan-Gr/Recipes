Sous Vide Tempered Chocolate

Ingredients

  • Chocolate
  • Water, for the water baths

Instructions

  1. Seal the chocolate in a plastic bag.
  2. Melt the chocolate by placing it in a water bath at 115 degrees for around 5 minutes. This can either be hot tap water or water warmed using a sous vide machine.
  3. Heat another pot of water to 81 degrees using the sous vide machine; as soon as the chocolate is melted, agitate it and transfer it to this water bath.
  4. Keep it in the 81 degree water bath until the chocolate has cooled down to 81 degrees.
  5. Set the sous vide to 90 degrees. Hold the chocolate at 90 degrees for 5 minutes, agitating every minute.
  6. Hold at 90 degrees until ready to use.
