Sous Vide Tempered Chocolate ✓ tried

From: Sous Vide Tempered Chocolate.docx

Seal chocolate in plastic bag. Melt chocolate by placing it in water bath at 115 degrees for around 5 minutes. This can either be hot tap water or water warmed using sous vide machine.

Heat another pot of water to 81 degrees using sous vide machine and as soon as chocolate is melted, agitate it and transfer it to this water bath.

Keep it in 81 degree water bath until chocolate has cooled down to 81 degrees.

Set sous vide to 90 degrees. Hold chocolate at 90 degrees for 5 minutes, agitating every minute.

Hold at 90 degrees until ready to use.
