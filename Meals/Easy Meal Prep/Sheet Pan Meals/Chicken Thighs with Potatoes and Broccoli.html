Sheet Pan: Chicken Thighs with Potatoes and Broccoli

Serves 4. Everything goes on the pan together: thighs, potatoes, and broccoli roast in 25 minutes at 425°F.

Ingredients

  • 600g (1.3 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 600g (1.3 lb) Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 300g (about 4 cups) broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 1 lemon: 2 tbsp (30g) juice
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large sheet pan (at least 13x18 inches) with foil.
  2. Toss the chicken thighs with 1 tbsp of the olive oil, the lemon juice, garlic, 1 tsp of the salt, 1/2 tsp of the pepper, and the oregano. Marinate 10 minutes (or up to 4 hours in the fridge).
  3. Toss the potatoes and broccoli with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper.
  4. Spread the potatoes on the pan, nestle in the chicken thighs, and scatter the broccoli around everything, keeping pieces in a single layer.
  5. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, tossing halfway, until the chicken reaches 175°F (80°C) and the potatoes are tender.
