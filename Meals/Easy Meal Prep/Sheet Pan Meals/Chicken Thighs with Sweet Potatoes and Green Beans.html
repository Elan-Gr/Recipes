Sheet Pan: Chicken Thighs with Sweet Potatoes and Green Beans

Serves 4. Sweet potatoes need the full bake; green beans go on the same pan and stay crisp-tender.

Ingredients

  • 600g (1.3 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 600g (1.3 lb) sweet potatoes, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
  • 250g (about 3 cups) green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large sheet pan with foil.
  2. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp of the olive oil, the garlic, 1 tsp of the salt, 1/2 tsp of the pepper, and the paprika. Marinate 10 minutes.
  3. Toss the sweet potatoes and green beans with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper.
  4. Spread everything on the pan in a single layer, thighs nestled among the vegetables.
  5. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, tossing halfway, until the chicken reaches 175°F (80°C) and the sweet potatoes are tender.
