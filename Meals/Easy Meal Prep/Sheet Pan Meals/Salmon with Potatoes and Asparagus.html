Sheet Pan: Salmon with Potatoes and Asparagus

Serves 4. Potatoes get a head start; salmon and asparagus need only 12 to 15 minutes at the end.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 600g / 1.3 lb total)
  • 600g (1.3 lb) Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 300g (about 1 bunch) asparagus, tough ends trimmed
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 1 lemon: 2 tbsp (30g) juice, plus wedges to serve
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried dill (or 1 tbsp fresh)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large sheet pan with foil.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp of the olive oil, 1 tsp of the salt, and 1/2 tsp of the pepper. Spread on the pan and bake 10 minutes.
  3. Rub the salmon with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, the lemon juice, garlic, dill, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper. Toss the asparagus with a drizzle of oil and a pinch of salt.
  4. Add the salmon (skin-side down) and asparagus to the pan. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the salmon flakes at 145°F (63°C) and the asparagus is crisp-tender.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.
