Sheet Pan: Salmon with Sweet Potatoes and Green Beans

Serves 4. Same split timing with sweet potatoes and green beans alongside the salmon.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 600g / 1.3 lb total)
  • 600g (1.3 lb) sweet potatoes, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
  • 250g (about 3 cups) green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large sheet pan with foil.
  2. Toss the sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp of the olive oil, 1 tsp of the salt, 1/2 tsp of the pepper, and the paprika. Spread on the pan and bake 10 minutes.
  3. Rub the salmon with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, the garlic, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper. Toss the green beans with a drizzle of oil and a pinch of salt.
  4. Add the salmon (skin-side down) and green beans to the pan. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the salmon flakes at 145°F (63°C).
  5. Serve immediately.
