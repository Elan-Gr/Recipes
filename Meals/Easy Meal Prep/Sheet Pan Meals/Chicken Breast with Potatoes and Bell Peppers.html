Sheet Pan: Chicken Breast with Potatoes and Bell Peppers

Serves 4. Timing split: potatoes start first, then the breast and peppers join for the last 15 minutes so the breast stays juicy.

Ingredients

  • 600g (1.3 lb) boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 600g (1.3 lb) Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2 bell peppers (300g), cut into strips
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 1 lemon: 2 tbsp (30g) juice
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large sheet pan with foil.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp of the olive oil, 1 tsp of the salt, and 1/2 tsp of the pepper. Spread on the pan and bake 10 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, toss the chicken breasts with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper. Toss the peppers with a drizzle of oil and a pinch of salt.
  4. Add the chicken and peppers to the pan. Bake 13 to 15 minutes more, until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) and the potatoes are tender.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, slice the chicken, and serve.
