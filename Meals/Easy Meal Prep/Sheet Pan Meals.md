On a sheet pan, put cut potatoes or sweet potatoes, marinated chicken thighs, chicken breast, or salmon, and vegetables. Bake at 425 degrees for 20-25 minutes, tossing half way through. If using salmon or chicken breast, add meat after 10 minutes and cook until meat is done
