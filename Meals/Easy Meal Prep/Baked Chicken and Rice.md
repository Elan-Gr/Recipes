Marinate boneless or bone in chicken thighs, drumsticks or chicken breast. If using chicken breast, cut into 1 inch cubes. In a baking dish, put oil, water, seasonings, and rice. Ratio of white rice to water should be enough to cook the rice. If using dark meat, chicken to baking dish. Add whatever vegetables you would like, after tossing them in oil and seasonings. Cover with foil and bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes if using white rice or 60 minutes if using brown rice. At this point, add chicken if using light meat. Uncover and bake for around 15-20 more minutes, or until chicken is cooked.

For 5 servings:

1.5 cups white rice, 1.5 cups water and 3 chicken breasts or 5 chicken thighs

or

1.5 cups brown rice and 3 cups water, and 3 chicken breasts or 5 chicken thighs
