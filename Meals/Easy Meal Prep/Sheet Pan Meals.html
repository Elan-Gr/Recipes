Sheet Pan Meals

Ingredients

  • potatoes or sweet potatoes, cut
  • chicken thighs, chicken breast, or salmon (marinated)
  • vegetables

Instructions

  1. On a sheet pan, put cut potatoes or sweet potatoes, marinated chicken thighs, chicken breast, or salmon, and vegetables.
  2. Bake at 425 degrees for 20-25 minutes, tossing half way through.
  3. If using salmon or chicken breast, add meat after 10 minutes and cook until meat is done.
