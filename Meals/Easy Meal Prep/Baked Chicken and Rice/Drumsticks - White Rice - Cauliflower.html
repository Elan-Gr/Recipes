Baked Chicken and Rice: Drumsticks, White Rice, and Cauliflower

Serves 5. Bone-in drumsticks flavor the rice as they bake; cauliflower florets join for the uncovered finish.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken drumsticks (about 900g / 2 lb)
  • 1 1/2 cups (280g) long-grain white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups (355g) water
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 350g (about 1 small head) cauliflower, cut into small florets
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt, divided
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (2g) paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Pat the drumsticks dry and season with 1 tsp of the salt, the pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
  3. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, stir together the rice, water, and 1 tbsp of the olive oil.
  4. Nestle the drumsticks into the rice. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 30 minutes.
  5. Toss the cauliflower with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt.
  6. Remove the dish from the oven, scatter the cauliflower around the drumsticks, and leave uncovered.
  7. Bake 20 to 25 minutes more, until the drumsticks reach 175°F (80°C) and the rice is tender.
  8. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
