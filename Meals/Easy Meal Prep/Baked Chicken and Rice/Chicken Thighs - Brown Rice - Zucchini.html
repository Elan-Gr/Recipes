Baked Chicken and Rice: Chicken Thighs, Brown Rice, and Zucchini

Serves 5. Brown rice and thighs share the long covered bake; zucchini goes in at the end so it does not turn to mush.

Ingredients

  • 5 boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 750g / 1.65 lb)
  • 1 1/2 cups (280g) long-grain brown rice
  • 3 cups (710g) water
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 350g (about 2 medium) zucchini, cut into half-moons 1/2 inch thick
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt, divided
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried thyme

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Season the chicken thighs with 1 tsp of the salt, the pepper, garlic powder, and thyme.
  3. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, stir together the brown rice, water, and 1 tbsp of the olive oil.
  4. Nestle the seasoned thighs into the rice. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 60 minutes.
  5. Toss the zucchini with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt.
  6. Remove the dish from the oven, scatter the zucchini around the thighs, and leave uncovered.
  7. Bake 15 to 20 minutes more, until the thighs reach 175°F (80°C) and the rice is tender.
  8. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
