Baked Chicken and Rice: Chicken Thighs, White Rice, and Green Beans

Serves 5. Dark meat goes into the dish from the start; the green beans join for the uncovered finish so they stay crisp-tender.

Ingredients

  • 5 boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 750g / 1.65 lb)
  • 1 1/2 cups (280g) long-grain white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups (355g) water
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 300g (about 4 cups) green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt, divided
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (2g) paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Season the chicken thighs with 1 tsp of the salt, the pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
  3. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, stir together the rice, water, and 1 tbsp of the olive oil.
  4. Nestle the seasoned thighs into the rice. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 30 minutes.
  5. Toss the green beans with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt.
  6. Remove the dish from the oven, scatter the green beans around the thighs, and leave uncovered.
  7. Bake 15 to 20 minutes more, until the thighs reach 175°F (80°C) and the rice is tender.
  8. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
