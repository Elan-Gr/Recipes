Baked Chicken and Rice: Chicken Breast, White Rice, and Broccoli

Serves 5. A complete one-dish meal: cubed chicken breast baked over seasoned white rice with broccoli florets added for the final stretch so they stay bright green.

Ingredients

  • 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 680g / 1.5 lb), cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) cubes
  • 1 1/2 cups (280g) long-grain white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups (355g) water
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 300g (about 4 cups) broccoli florets
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt, divided
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (2g) paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a 9x13-inch (23x33 cm) baking dish, stir together the rice, water, 1 tbsp of the olive oil, 1 tsp of the salt, the pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
  3. Cover the dish tightly with foil and bake for 30 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, toss the broccoli florets with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt, and season the cubed chicken with the remaining 1/2 tsp salt.
  5. Remove the dish from the oven, scatter the chicken cubes and broccoli evenly over the rice, and re-cover with foil.
  6. Bake 10 minutes covered, then uncover and bake 10 to 15 minutes more, until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) and the rice is tender.
  7. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
