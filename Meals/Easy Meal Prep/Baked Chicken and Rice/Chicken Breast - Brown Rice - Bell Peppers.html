Baked Chicken and Rice: Chicken Breast, Brown Rice, and Bell Peppers

Serves 5. Nutty brown rice needs a longer bake; the chicken and peppers go in for the last stretch so neither overcooks.

Ingredients

  • 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 680g / 1.5 lb), cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) cubes
  • 1 1/2 cups (280g) long-grain brown rice
  • 3 cups (710g) water
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil, divided
  • 2 bell peppers (about 300g), sliced into strips
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt, divided
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (2g) paprika
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, stir together the brown rice, water, 1 tbsp of the olive oil, 1 tsp of the salt, the pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and oregano.
  3. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 60 minutes.
  4. Toss the bell pepper strips with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt; season the cubed chicken with the remaining 1/2 tsp salt.
  5. Remove the dish from the oven, scatter the chicken and peppers over the rice, and re-cover with foil.
  6. Bake 10 minutes covered, then uncover and bake 10 to 15 minutes more, until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) and the rice is tender.
  7. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
