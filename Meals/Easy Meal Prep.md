## Lunches and Dinners

### Option 1: Baked Chicken and Rice Dish

Base: Seasoned white rice

Protein: Chicken breast, chicken thighs

Vegetable: Broccoli, Carrots, zucchini, spinach, kale, bell peppers, brussels sprouts

### Option 2: Flatbread

Bake a simple flatbread at the beginning of the week and then put toppings on it and re-bake it

- Mozzarella, tomato, basil, olive
- Spinach and feta
- Mushroom and goat cheese
- Four cheese
- Mozzarella, artichokes, olives, roasted red peppers
- Pesto, mozzarella, cherry tomatoes, basil, balsamic
- Ricotta, mozzarella, roasted broccoli, garlic, lemon zest
- Chicken or beef, tahini, tomatoes, parsley
- Turkey, cranberry relish, arugula
- Harissa-marinated chicken, roasted eggplant, parsley
- Smoked salmon, cream cheese, capers, red onion, dill
- Olive oil, za'atar

### Option 3: Sheet Pan Meals

Base: Potatoes or sweet potatoes

Meat: Chicken breast, chicken thighs, fish, tofu, meatloaf

Vegetables: Broccoli, Carrots, zucchini, spinach, kale, bell peppers, brussels sprouts, asparagus

### Option 4: Beef Stew

Base: Barley

Meat: Beef

Vegetables: Carrots, Kale, spinach

### Option 5: Curry

Base: Potatoes, rice

Meat: Beef, chicken, tofu

Vegetables: Broccoli, Carrots, zucchini, spinach, bell peppers

### Option 6: Salad

Base: Kale or spinach

Grain: Wild rice, brown Rice, white rice, or quinoa

Meat: Beef, chicken, tofu, salmon

Vegetables: Shredded Carrots, halved cherry tomatoes, shredded cabbage, roasted broccoli, mushrooms

Toppings: Cheese, almonds,

Dressing: Pesto vinaigrette (1 cup tightly chopped flat leaf parsley leaves, 1/2 cup olive oil, 2 Tablespoons red wine vinegar, 1 Tablespoon dijon mustard, 2 cloves garlic, 1 teaspoon salt)

### Option 7: Shakshuka

Base: Bread

Meat: eggs

Vegetables: tomatoes, peppers

Stew tomatoes and peppers in covered or uncovered baking dish at 350 degrees with paprika and cumin, add eggs and cook eggs uncovered for 20 minutes or so. Serve with bread

### Option 8: Sandwiches

Base: Bread

Meat: chicken, beef

Vegetables: Spinach, kale

Sauces: Mustard, ketchup, mayo, etc.

### Option 9: Pastas

Base: Pasta

Meat: chicken, meatballs, stewed beef

Vegetables: broccoli, carrots, spinach

Sauces: Tomato sauce
