Ground Meat: Turkey Burgers

Makes 4 burgers. Turkey needs oil and spices to make up for the missing fat — do not skip them.

Ingredients

  • 680g (1 1/2 lb) ground turkey
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (2g) smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 4 burger buns

Instructions

  1. Combine the turkey, olive oil, salt, garlic powder, paprika, and pepper. Mix gently just until combined.
  2. Form into 4 patties (170g each), slightly wider than the buns, with a shallow dimple in the center.
  3. Cook in an oiled skillet or on a grill over medium heat 5 to 6 minutes per side, until cooked through to 165°F (74°C).
  4. Rest 3 minutes, then serve on buns.
