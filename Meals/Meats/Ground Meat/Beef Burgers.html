Ground Meat: Beef Burgers

Makes 4 burgers. High-fat beef needs nothing but salt — the fat is the flavor. Form the patties wider than you think; they shrink.

Ingredients

  • 680g (1 1/2 lb) ground beef (80/20)
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 4 burger buns

Instructions

  1. Divide the beef into 4 equal portions (170g each). Form into patties slightly wider than your buns, with a shallow dimple in the center of each. Handle as little as possible.
  2. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Cook in a hot cast-iron skillet or on a grill over medium-high heat, 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium (160°F / 71°C center).
  4. Rest 3 minutes, then serve on buns.
