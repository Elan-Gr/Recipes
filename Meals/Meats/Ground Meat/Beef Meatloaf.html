Ground Meat: Beef Meatloaf

Serves 6. A straightforward loaf: well-seasoned, packed into a pan, baked low and slow. Rest before slicing.

Ingredients

  • 900g (2 lb) ground beef (80/20)
  • 80g (about 3/4 cup) traditional breadcrumbs
  • 2 large eggs (100g), beaten
  • 1 onion (150g), finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves (6g), minced
  • 15g (1/4 cup) chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil, for the pan

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Oil a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Combine all ingredients except the oil in a large bowl. Mix gently just until combined.
  3. Pack into the loaf pan, smoothing the top.
  4. Bake about 1 1/2 hours, until the center reads 160°F (71°C).
  5. Rest 10 minutes, then slice and serve.
