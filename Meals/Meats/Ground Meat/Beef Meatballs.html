Ground Meat: Beef Meatballs

Makes about 10 meatballs; serves 4. Handle the meat as little as possible for tender meatballs. Lots of herbs, as the parent page insists.

Ingredients

  • 450g (1 lb) ground beef (80/20)
  • 60g (about 1/2 cup) traditional breadcrumbs (not panko)
  • 1 large egg (50g), beaten
  • 15g (1/4 cup) chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves (6g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil, for the pan

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Oil a sheet pan with the olive oil.
  2. In a bowl, combine the beef, breadcrumbs, egg, parsley, garlic, salt, and pepper. Mix gently with your hands just until combined — do not overwork.
  3. Form into 10 balls (about 55g each) and arrange on the pan.
  4. Bake 15 to 18 minutes, until browned and cooked through to 160°F (71°C).
