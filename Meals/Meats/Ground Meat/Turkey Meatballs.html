Ground Meat: Turkey Meatballs

Makes about 10 meatballs; serves 4. Turkey is leaner, so a spoon of oil goes into the mix to keep them moist.

Ingredients

  • 450g (1 lb) ground turkey
  • 60g (about 1/2 cup) traditional breadcrumbs (not panko)
  • 1 large egg (50g), beaten
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil, plus more for the pan
  • 15g (1/4 cup) chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves (6g), minced
  • 1 tsp (3g) dried oregano
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Oil a sheet pan.
  2. Combine the turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, 1 tbsp olive oil, parsley, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Mix gently just until combined.
  3. Form into 10 balls and arrange on the pan.
  4. Bake 15 to 18 minutes, until cooked through to 165°F (74°C).
