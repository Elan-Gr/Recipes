Spatchcocked Chicken

Ingredients

  • whole chicken

Instructions

  1. Spatchcock the chicken by cutting along both sides of the bone on the bottom of the chicken (the backbone) with kitchen shears. Press down on the top of the chicken to flatten it.
  2. Place chicken on an oven-safe rack on a baking sheet.
  3. Roast at 425 degrees for about 45-50 minutes.
