Steak: Pan-Sautéed Ribeye, Medium

Serves 2. A ribeye taken to medium in the pan — the extra fat keeps it juicy at higher doneness.

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks, about 1 inch thick (about 280g / 10 oz each)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) avocado oil or other high-smoke-point oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Pat the steaks dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  3. Sear 4 to 5 minutes per side until the center reads 135°F (57°C).
  4. Rest 5 minutes (carryover to about 140°F / 60°C) and serve.
