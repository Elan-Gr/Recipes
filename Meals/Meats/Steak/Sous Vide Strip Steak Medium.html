Steak: Sous Vide Strip Steak, Medium

Serves 2. Same sous vide method at 140°F for a true medium — warm pink center, no gray band.

Ingredients

  • 2 strip steaks, 1 1/2 inches thick (about 300g / 10 oz each)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) avocado oil or other high-smoke-point oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Set the sous vide circulator to 140°F (60°C).
  2. Season the steaks with salt and pepper and seal in bags.
  3. Cook 2 hours.
  4. Pat completely dry. Sear in smoking-hot oil 60 to 90 seconds per side.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, slice, and serve.
