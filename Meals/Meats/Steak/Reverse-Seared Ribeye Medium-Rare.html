Steak: Reverse-Seared Ribeye, Medium-Rare

Serves 2. Low oven first for even doneness, then a ripping-hot sear. No special equipment beyond a thermometer.

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks, 1 1/2 inches thick (about 300g / 10 oz each)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) avocado oil or other high-smoke-point oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 250°F (120°C). Set a wire rack on a sheet pan.
  2. Season the steaks with salt and pepper and place on the rack.
  3. Roast until an instant-read thermometer in the center reads 115°F (46°C), about 25 to 30 minutes.
  4. Heat the oil in a cast-iron skillet over high heat until just smoking. Sear the steaks 90 seconds per side.
  5. Rest 5 minutes (carryover brings it to about 130°F / 54°C), slice, and serve.
