Steak: Reverse-Seared Strip Steak, Medium

Serves 2. Reverse sear taken to 125°F in the oven for a finished medium after searing and resting.

Ingredients

  • 2 strip steaks, 1 1/2 inches thick (about 300g / 10 oz each)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) avocado oil or other high-smoke-point oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 250°F (120°C). Set a wire rack on a sheet pan.
  2. Season the steaks and place on the rack.
  3. Roast until the center reads 125°F (52°C), about 30 to 35 minutes.
  4. Sear in smoking-hot oil 90 seconds per side.
  5. Rest 5 minutes (carryover to about 140°F / 60°C), slice, and serve.
