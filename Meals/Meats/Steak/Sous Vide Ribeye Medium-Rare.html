Steak: Sous Vide Ribeye, Medium-Rare

Serves 2. Two 1 1/2-inch ribeyes cooked edge-to-edge medium-rare, then seared hard for crust. Needs a sous vide circulator.

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks, 1 1/2 inches thick (about 300g / 10 oz each)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) avocado oil or other high-smoke-point oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Set the sous vide circulator to 130°F (54°C).
  2. Season the steaks generously with salt and pepper. Seal in bags (vacuum or water-displacement method).
  3. Cook 2 hours (up to 4 hours is fine).
  4. Remove, pat completely dry with paper towels — a dry surface is essential for searing.
  5. Heat the oil in a cast-iron skillet over high heat until just smoking. Sear 60 to 90 seconds per side, including the fat cap edge.
  6. Rest 5 minutes, slice, and serve.
