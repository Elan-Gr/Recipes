Teriyaki Roasted Tofu

Ingredients

  • tofu
  • soy sauce
  • honey
  • garlic
  • onion powder
  • teriyaki sauce

Instructions

  1. Cut up tofu and let it sit in a bag with some soy sauce, honey, garlic, onion powder, and teriyaki sauce for 30 minutes to an hour.
  2. Spread tofu out on a baking sheet, making sure it is dry.
  3. Bake at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for about 20 minutes.
