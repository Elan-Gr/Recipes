Fried Chicken

Ingredients

  • 225 g flour
  • salt
  • paprika
  • pepper
  • 2 egg whites
  • 240 g water
  • chicken breasts
  • oil, for deep frying

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, gently mix 225 g flour, salt, paprika, pepper, 2 egg whites, and 240 g water. Add more water if necessary to get a batter consistency.
  2. Cut up chicken breasts and place in batter. Let marinate for 1 hour (or dry brine chicken and then just dip into batter).
  3. After done marinating, mix flour, salt, and paprika. Dredge chicken in flour mixture and deep fry at 350 degrees until golden brown.
