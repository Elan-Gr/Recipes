Chicken Breast

Roasted Chicken Breast

Ingredients

  • chicken breasts
  • oil
  • seasonings

Instructions

  1. Cut each chicken breast into 2 or 3 pieces.
  2. Coat in oil and seasonings, or preferably, marinate.
  3. Bake at 450 degrees Fahrenheit for about 20-25 minutes.
  4. It is easier to cut the chicken into pieces before cooking it. In this case, you can bring the cooking time down to about 10 minutes.
  5. One serving is about 7 oz of chicken. Two large (10 oz) sliced chicken breasts is good for three meals.
