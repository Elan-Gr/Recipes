Ground Meat

Meatloaf (or gyro, ect.)

  • Season meat well and wrap in foil or pack into a loaf pan. Bake at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about an hour and a half
  • Add up to 40 grams of bread crumbs per pound of ground meat for softer texture.
  • Add up to 1 teaspoon (5 grams) kosher salt

Meatballs

  • Bake at 375 for about 12-20 minutes
  • Add up to 60 grams of breadcrumbs (traditional, not panko) per pound of ground meat for softer texture
  • Make sure to use lots of herbs
  • One pound of ground beef makes about 9-12 meatballs
  • Each serving is about a third a pound

Hamburgers

  • Use 1/3 lb ground meat per burger for traditional burgers or 1/4 lb for smash burgers
  • Flatten out larger than you think you will need (they will shrink) and cook in frying pan or grill
  • For beef, use as high fat percentage as possible. The fat has enough flavor, so season with just salt
  • For turkey, the meat doesn't have as much fat so you should add oil. Add x g oil per lb. Also recommend seasoning with spices

(When cooking with ground meat, handle the ground meat as little as possible because the more you handle it, the tougher it will be. Also, it is best to put the meat in the refrigerator for a few hours after mixing to let the breadcrumbs absorb into the meat.)

Beef Roasts

Braised Beef (good for short ribs, chuck roast, ect.)

  • Cook in the oven at 300-325 degrees Fahrenheit for 3 to 4 hours, with braising liquid about a third of the way up the meat. Braise until beef reaches temperature of 195-205, which is when the collagen starts to break down. The beef may stall at about 165 degrees for a while because this is where a lot of the collagen breaks down happens. Smaller amounts of meat will cook faster, so cook them at the lower temperature if you want to make sure they have enough time to braise and are very soft.

Roasted Beef (good for sirloin tip roast, chuck roast)

  • Coat with oil and season
  • Roast at 450-500 degrees Fahrenheit for 15 minutes, and lower the temperature to 300 degrees. Cook at 250-300 degrees for another 40 to 75 minutes until desired doneness (around 145 degrees for medium).

Brisket (Braised)

  • Trim most of the fat of the brisket, leaving a small layer of fat on top
  • Make Braising Liquid (12 oz Coke, 1 envelope onion soup mix, 1 cup chili sauce)
  • Pour liquid 1/3 the way up the brisket in roasting pan
  • Cover tightly in roasting pan and bake for 1 hour per pound at 200-250 degrees F until it reaches around 195 degrees. Smaller amounts of meat will cook faster, so cook them at the lower temperature if you want to make sure they have enough time to braise and are very soft. The goal is to hold the temperature at between 165-180 degrees until you achieve the desired tenderness, so you may need to lower the temperature and do this for longer.
  • Can also do this like a more traditional brisket without any braising liquid. Just coat the brisket in salt and black pepper, put it in a roasting pan, and cover tightly. For the last 15 or 20 minutes, remove from foil, add more of the rub, and place on roasting tray, increase oven to 350 degrees, and develop a bark untried

Chicken Breast

Roasted Chicken Breast

  • Cut each chicken breast into 2 or 3 pieces
  • Coat in oil and seasonings, or preferably, marinate
  • Bake at 450 degrees Fahrenheit for about 20-25 minutes
  • It is easier to cut the chicken into pieces before cooking it
  • In this case, you can bring the cooking time down to about 10 minutes
  • One serving is about 7 oz of chicken
  • Two large (10 oz) sliced chicken breasts is good for three meals.

Seared Chicken Breast

  • Cut Chicken breasts into medium pieces and cook on medium heat.

Chicken Thighs and Legs

Oven Roasted Chicken Legs and Chicken Thighs

  • Coat in oil and seasonings, or preferably, marinate. Roast at 420 degrees Fahrenheit for about 25 min, or a little bit longer if bone-in. If your meat has skin, cook it with the skin side up, and you can broil it for a few minutes at the end to crisp up the skin.
  • One boneless skinless chicken breast is good for one meal

Pan-Seared Chicken Legs and Chicken Thighs

  • Cook at medium-low heat, covered, and with the skin-side down

Braised Chicken Legs and Chicken Thighs

  • Bake in braising liquid at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 2.5 hours (2 hours covered, 30 minutes uncovered)
  • Liquid should come about a third of the way up the chicken thighs.

Whole Chicken

Spatchcocked Chicken

  • Spatchcock the chicken by cutting along both sides of the bone on the bottom of the chicken (the backbone) with kitchen shears. Press down on the top of the chicken to flatten it.
  • Place chicken on an oven-safe rack on a baking sheet.
  • Roast at 425 degrees for about 45-50 minutes.

Whole Chicken

  • Roast at 325 degrees for x minutes untried

Turkey

Turkey Breast

  • Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days
  • Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound untried

Whole Turkey

  • Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days
  • Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound. Roast uncovered, but cover in tin foil if skin is getting too dark.

Spatchcocked whole turkey

  • Brine or dry brine turkey
  • Roast at 450 for about 80 minutes untried

Fish

Salmon Filet

  • Bake at 400 degrees Fahrenheit until desired doneness
  • Sautee
  • Broil at high heat for a couple of minutes. This will cook the fish quickly and preserve some of the moisture, but is a less delicate method of cooking.

Salmon Side

  • Bake at 350 for about 25-35 minutes

Tilapia

  • Bake at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 13 minutes
  • Sautee
  • Broil at high heat for a couple of minutes. This will cook the fish quickly and preserve some of the moisture, but is a less delicate method of cooking.

Whole Fish

  • Cut a few slits through the skin from the top of the fish to the belly
  • Rub with oil and season
  • Stuff belly if desired
  • Place on baking sheet
  • Roast at 450 degrees for 15-20 minutes until desired doneness

Tofu

Teriyaki Roasted Tofu

  • Cut up tofu and let it sit in a bag with some soy sauce, honey, garlic, onion powder, and teriyaki sauce for 30 minutes to an hour.
  • Spread tofu out on a baking sheet, making sure it is dry
  • Bake at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for about 20 minutes

Steak

Sous Vide Steak (Good for thick cuts of steak)

  • Sous vide for 1-6 hours at 125 degrees for rare, 130 degrees for medium rare, 140 degrees for medium, 150 degrees for medium well, and 155 degrees for well done.
  • Sear in very hot pan with some high smoke point oil

Reverse sear (Good for thick cuts of steak)

  • First sear the steak at 250 degrees until it reaches desired temperature

| Doneness | Target Temperature in the Oven | Final Target Temperature | Approximate Time in Oven | |---|---|---|---| | Rare | 105°F (40°C) | 120°F (49°C) | 20 to 25 minutes | | Medium-Rare | 115°F (46°C) | 130°F (54°C) | 25 to 30 minutes | | Medium | 125°F (52°C) | 140°F (60°C) | 30 to 35 minutes | | Medium-Well | 135°F (57°C) | 150°F (66°C) | 35 to 40 minutes |

(For 1 1/2-inch steaks in a 250°F / 120°C oven)

  • Then, sear in a very hot pan or on the grill with some high smoke point oil

Saute (Good for thinner cuts of steak)

  • Cook in pan on medium-high heat with some oil until desired doneness. Let rest after cooking.

Tips

Dry spices will burn at temperatures above around 375 degrees, so if you need to cook above 375 degrees, consider using a marinade. Marinade should have oil, flavorings, salt, and maybe some acid. Let marinade for at least a couple of hours, or overnight.
