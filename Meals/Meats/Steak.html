Sous Vide Steak

Good for thick cuts of steak.

Ingredients

  • steak
  • high smoke point oil

Instructions

  1. Sous vide for 1-6 hours at 125 degrees for rare, 130 degrees for medium rare, 140 degrees for medium, 150 degrees for medium well, and 155 degrees for well done.
  2. Sear in very hot pan with some high smoke point oil.

Reverse Sear

Good for thick cuts of steak.

Ingredients

  • steak
  • high smoke point oil

| Doneness | Target Temperature in the Oven | Final Target Temperature | Approximate Time in Oven | |---|---|---|---| | Rare | 105°F (40°C) | 120°F (49°C) | 20 to 25 minutes | | Medium-Rare | 115°F (46°C) | 130°F (54°C) | 25 to 30 minutes | | Medium | 125°F (52°C) | 140°F (60°C) | 30 to 35 minutes | | Medium-Well | 135°F (57°C) | 150°F (66°C) | 35 to 40 minutes |

(For 1 1/2-inch steaks in a 250°F / 120°C oven)

Instructions

  1. First sear the steak at 250 degrees until it reaches desired temperature.
  2. Then, sear in a very hot pan or on the grill with some high smoke point oil.

Saute

Good for thinner cuts of steak.

Ingredients

  • steak
  • oil

Instructions

  1. Cook in pan on medium-high heat with some oil until desired doneness. Let rest after cooking.
