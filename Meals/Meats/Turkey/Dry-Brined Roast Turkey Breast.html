Turkey: Dry-Brined Roast Turkey Breast

Serves 6 to 8. Dry brining gives crispier skin than wet brining — salt the breast uncovered in the fridge for 2 days.

Ingredients

  • 1 bone-in turkey breast (about 1.8 kg / 4 lb)
  • 18g (1 tbsp) kosher salt, for the dry brine
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried thyme

Instructions

  1. Two days before: rub the breast all over with the salt. Place uncovered on a rack over a tray and refrigerate 1 to 3 days.
  2. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Pat the breast dry (do not rinse). Rub with the olive oil, pepper, and thyme.
  3. Roast on a rack 70 to 80 minutes, until the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C).
  4. Rest 15 minutes before carving.
