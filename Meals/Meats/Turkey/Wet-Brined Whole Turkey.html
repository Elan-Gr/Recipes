Turkey: Wet-Brined Whole Turkey

Serves 10 to 12. The full bird, wet-brined for maximum juiciness. You need a container (or brining bag) big enough to hold it.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole turkey (about 5.5 kg / 12 lb), giblets removed
  • 145g (8 tbsp) kosher salt, for the brine
  • 1.9 kg (8 cups) water, for the brine
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (6g) black pepper
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried thyme

Instructions

  1. The day before: dissolve the salt in the water. Submerge the turkey in the brine, cover, and refrigerate 16 to 24 hours.
  2. Remove, rinse, and pat completely dry, inside and out.
  3. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Rub the turkey with the olive oil, pepper, and thyme. Tuck the wing tips under.
  4. Roast 15 to 20 minutes per pound — about 3 to 3 1/2 hours for a 12-lb bird — until the breast reads 165°F (74°C) and the thigh reads 175°F (80°C). Tent with foil if the skin darkens.
  5. Rest 30 minutes before carving.
