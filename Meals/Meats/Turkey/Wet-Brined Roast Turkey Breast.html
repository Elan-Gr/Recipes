Turkey: Wet-Brined Roast Turkey Breast

Serves 6 to 8. A bone-in breast brined overnight in salt water roasts up juicy. Start the brine the day before.

Ingredients

  • 1 bone-in turkey breast (about 1.8 kg / 4 lb)
  • 72g (4 tbsp) kosher salt, for the brine
  • 950g (4 cups) water, for the brine
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried thyme

Instructions

  1. The day before: dissolve the salt in the water in a container large enough to hold the breast. Submerge the breast, cover, and refrigerate 16 to 24 hours.
  2. Remove the breast, rinse, and pat completely dry.
  3. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Rub the breast with the olive oil, pepper, and thyme.
  4. Roast on a rack in a roasting pan 70 to 80 minutes, until the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C). Tent with foil if the skin darkens too fast.
  5. Rest 15 minutes before carving.
