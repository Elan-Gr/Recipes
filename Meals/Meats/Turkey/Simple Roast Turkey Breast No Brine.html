Turkey: Simple Roast Turkey Breast (No Brine)

Serves 4 to 6. No advance planning needed: a smaller boneless breast with a garlic-herb oil rub, roasted in about an hour.

Ingredients

  • 1 boneless turkey breast (about 1.4 kg / 3 lb)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), minced
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried thyme
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried rosemary, crushed
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Pat the breast dry. Rub all over with the olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
  3. Place on a rack in a roasting pan and roast about 60 minutes, until the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C). Tent with foil if browning too fast.
  4. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.
