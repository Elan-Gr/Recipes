Turkey: Spatchcocked Turkey

Serves 10 to 12. Removing the backbone lets the bird lie flat: faster, more even cooking, and every bit of skin gets crisp.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole turkey (about 5.5 kg / 12 lb), giblets removed
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 2 tbsp (36g) kosher salt
  • 2 tsp (6g) black pepper
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried thyme

Instructions

  1. With sturdy kitchen shears, cut along both sides of the backbone and remove it (save for stock). Flip the turkey breast-side up and press hard on the breastbone until it cracks and lies flat.
  2. Rub all over with the olive oil, salt, pepper, and thyme.
  3. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Place flat on a rack over a rimmed sheet pan.
  4. Roast about 80 minutes, until the breast reads 165°F (74°C) and the skin is deep golden.
  5. Rest 30 minutes before carving.
