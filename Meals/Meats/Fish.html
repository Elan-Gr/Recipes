Salmon Filet

Ingredients

  • salmon filet

Instructions

  1. Bake at 400 degrees Fahrenheit until desired doneness.
  2. Alternatively, sautee.
  3. Alternatively, broil at high heat for a couple of minutes. This will cook the fish quickly and preserve some of the moisture, but is a less delicate method of cooking.

Whole Fish

Ingredients

  • whole fish
  • oil
  • seasonings

Instructions

  1. Cut a few slits through the skin from the top of the fish to the belly.
  2. Rub with oil and season.
  3. Stuff belly if desired.
  4. Place on baking sheet.
  5. Roast at 450 degrees for 15-20 minutes until desired doneness.
