Turkey Breast

Ingredients

  • turkey breast
  • salt
  • water

Instructions

  1. Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days.
  2. Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound. untried

Whole Turkey

Ingredients

  • whole turkey
  • salt
  • water

Instructions

  1. Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days.
  2. Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound. Roast uncovered, but cover in tin foil if skin is getting too dark.

Spatchcocked whole turkey

Instructions
