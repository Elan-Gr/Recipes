Turkey

Turkey Breast

  • Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days
  • Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound untried

Whole Turkey

  • Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days
  • Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound. Roast uncovered, but cover in tin foil if skin is getting too dark.

Spatchcocked whole turkey

  • Brine or dry brine turkey
  • Roast at 450 for about 80 minutes untried
