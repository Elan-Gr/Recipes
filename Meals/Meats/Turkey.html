Turkey Breast

Ingredients

  • turkey breast
  • salt
  • water

Instructions

  1. Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days.
  2. Lightly oil the turkey.
  3. Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound. untried

Whole Turkey

Ingredients

  • whole turkey
  • salt
  • water

Instructions

  1. Brine turkey in salt water (4 tablespoons salt / 4 cups water) for 16-24 hours, or dry brine in salt uncovered in the fridge for 1-3 days.
  2. Lightly oil the turkey.
  3. Roast at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 15-20 minutes per pound. Roast uncovered, but cover in tin foil if skin is getting too dark.

Spatchcocked whole turkey

Ingredients

  • whole turkey
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Brine or dry brine turkey.
  2. Lightly oil the turkey.
  3. Roast at 450 for about 80 minutes. untried

Thermometer placement

  • Thickest part of the breast: insert the thermometer horizontally or from the side into the deepest, meatiest portion of the breast, near the neck, avoiding the bone. Should read 160°F.
  • Innermost part of the thigh: insert the thermometer into the thickest part of the thigh meat, away from the bone. Should read 165–180°F.
