# Chicken Wings

Ingredients:

- 5 g (1 teaspoon) baking powder
- 5 g (1 teaspoon) kosher salt
- 1 lb chicken wings

Directions:

1. Coat chicken wings in baking powder and salt
2. Reccomended: Let sit uncovered on a rack (or at least on some paper towel) for 10-24 hours to let the skin dry out.
3. Bake at 450 degrees for 20 minutes on each side on a wire rack
4. Toss in sauce
