Chicken Thighs and Legs

Oven Roasted Chicken Legs and Chicken Thighs

Ingredients

  • chicken legs and chicken thighs
  • oil
  • seasonings

Instructions

  1. Coat in oil and seasonings, or preferably, marinate.
  2. Roast at 420 degrees Fahrenheit for about 25 min, or a little bit longer if bone-in.
  3. If your meat has skin, cook it with the skin side up, and you can broil it for a few minutes at the end to crisp up the skin.
  4. One boneless skinless chicken breast is good for one meal.

Pan-Seared Chicken Legs and Chicken Thighs

Ingredients

  • chicken legs and chicken thighs

Instructions

  1. Cook at medium-low heat, covered, and with the skin-side down.

Braised Chicken Legs and Chicken Thighs

Ingredients

  • chicken legs and chicken thighs
  • braising liquid

Instructions

  1. Bake in braising liquid at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 2.5 hours (2 hours covered, 30 minutes uncovered).
  2. Liquid should come about a third of the way up the chicken thighs.
