Beef Roasts

Braised Beef

Good for short ribs, chuck roast, ect.

Ingredients

  • beef
  • braising liquid

Instructions

  1. Cook in the oven at 300-325 degrees Fahrenheit for 3 to 4 hours, with braising liquid about a third of the way up the meat.
  2. Braise until beef reaches temperature of 195-205, which is when the collagen starts to break down. The beef may stall at about 165 degrees for a while because this is where a lot of the collagen breaks down happens.
  3. Smaller amounts of meat will cook faster, so cook them at the lower temperature if you want to make sure they have enough time to braise and are very soft.

Roasted Beef

Good for sirloin tip roast, chuck roast.

Ingredients

  • beef
  • oil
  • seasonings

Instructions

  1. Coat with oil and season.
  2. Roast at 450-500 degrees Fahrenheit for 15 minutes, and lower the temperature to 300 degrees. Cook at 250-300 degrees for another 40 to 75 minutes until desired doneness (around 145 degrees for medium).

Brisket (Braised)

Ingredients

  • brisket
  • 12 oz Coke
  • 1 envelope onion soup mix
  • 1 cup chili sauce
  • salt
  • black pepper

Instructions

  1. Trim most of the fat of the brisket, leaving a small layer of fat on top.
  2. Make braising liquid: 12 oz Coke, 1 envelope onion soup mix, 1 cup chili sauce.
  3. Pour liquid 1/3 the way up the brisket in roasting pan.
  4. Cover tightly in roasting pan and bake for 1 hour per pound at 200-250 degrees F until it reaches around 195 degrees. Smaller amounts of meat will cook faster, so cook them at the lower temperature if you want to make sure they have enough time to braise and are very soft. The goal is to hold the temperature at between 165-180 degrees until you achieve the desired tenderness, so you may need to lower the temperature and do this for longer.
  5. Alternatively, do a more traditional brisket without any braising liquid: coat the brisket in salt and black pepper, put it in a roasting pan, and cover tightly.
