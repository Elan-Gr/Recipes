Fish: Sautéed Tilapia

Serves 4. Three minutes a side in a hot pan gives crisp edges on thin filets. Do not crowd the pan.

Ingredients

  • 4 tilapia filets (about 500g / 1.1 lb total)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Instructions

  1. Pat the filets dry and season with the salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  3. Cook the filets 3 minutes per side (in two batches if needed) until golden and flaky at 145°F (63°C).
  4. Serve with lemon wedges.
