Fish: Roasted Whole Fish

Serves 2 to 3. A whole branzino or sea bass, slashed and stuffed with lemon and herbs, roasted hot and fast.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole branzino or sea bass (about 1 kg / 2.2 lb), scaled and gutted
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 4 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), smashed
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Cut 3 or 4 slits through the skin on each side of the fish, from top to belly.
  3. Rub the fish inside and out with the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Stuff the cavity with the lemon slices, thyme, and garlic.
  4. Roast 15 to 20 minutes, until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily and the thickest part reads 145°F (63°C).
  5. Serve whole and fillet at the table, or lift the fillets off the bones in the kitchen.
