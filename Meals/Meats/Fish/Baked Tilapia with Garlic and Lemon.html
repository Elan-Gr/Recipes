Fish: Baked Tilapia with Garlic and Lemon

Serves 4. Thin tilapia filets need only 13 minutes at 350°F; garlic and lemon keep the mild fish interesting.

Ingredients

  • 4 tilapia filets (about 500g / 1.1 lb total)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 1 lemon: 1 tbsp (15g) juice
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking dish with foil.
  2. Pat the filets dry and rub with the olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bake about 13 minutes, until the fish flakes easily and reads 145°F (63°C).
  4. Serve immediately.
