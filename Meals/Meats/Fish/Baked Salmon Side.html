Fish: Baked Salmon Side

Serves 6 to 8. One whole side of salmon for a crowd — low, gentle heat keeps it silky.

Ingredients

  • 1 side of salmon (about 900g / 2 lb), skin on
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 2 tbsp (8g) chopped fresh dill
  • 1 lemon: 1 tbsp (15g) juice
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Pat the salmon dry and rub with the olive oil, dill, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, until the thickest part reads 145°F (63°C) and the fish flakes.
  4. Rest 5 minutes, then cut into portions and serve.
