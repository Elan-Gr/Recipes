Fish: Broiled Salmon Filet

Serves 4. Under the broiler the top caramelizes while the center stays moist — six to eight minutes total.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon filets (about 600g / 1.3 lb total)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 tsp (3g) paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Position an oven rack 6 inches from the broiler and heat the broiler on high. Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Pat the filets dry and rub with the olive oil, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. Broil 6 to 8 minutes, without turning, until the top is browned and the center reads 145°F (63°C).
  4. Rest 2 minutes and serve.
