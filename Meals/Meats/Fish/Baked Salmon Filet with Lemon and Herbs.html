Fish: Baked Salmon Filet with Lemon and Herbs

Serves 4. The weeknight workhorse: salmon filets baked at 400°F until just opaque. Do not overcook — pull at 145°F.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon filets (about 600g / 1.3 lb total)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 lemon: 1 tbsp (15g) juice, plus wedges to serve
  • 2 tbsp (8g) chopped fresh dill (or 2 tsp dried)
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Pat the filets dry and rub with the olive oil, lemon juice, dill, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the fish flakes easily and the center reads 145°F (63°C).
  4. Serve with lemon wedges.
