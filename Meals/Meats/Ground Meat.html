Ground Meat

Meatloaf (or gyro, ect.)

  • Season meat well and wrap in foil or pack into a loaf pan. Bake at 325-350 degrees Fahrenheit for about an hour and a half
  • Add up to 40 grams of bread crumbs per pound of ground meat for softer texture.
  • Add up to 1 teaspoon (5 grams) kosher salt

Meatballs

  • Bake at 375 for about 12-20 minutes
  • Add up to 60 grams of breadcrumbs (traditional, not panko) per pound of ground meat for softer texture
  • Make sure to use lots of herbs
  • One pound of ground beef makes about 9-12 meatballs
  • Each serving is about a third a pound

Hamburgers

  • Use 1/3 lb ground meat per burger for traditional burgers or 1/4 lb for smash burgers
  • Flatten out larger than you think you will need (they will shrink) and cook in frying pan or grill
  • For beef, use as high fat percentage as possible. The fat has enough flavor, so season with just salt
  • For turkey, the meat doesn't have as much fat so you should add oil. Add x g oil per lb. Also recommend seasoning with spices

(When cooking with ground meat, handle the ground meat as little as possible because the more you handle it, the tougher it will be. Also, it is best to put the meat in the refrigerator for a few hours after mixing to let the breadcrumbs absorb into the meat.)
