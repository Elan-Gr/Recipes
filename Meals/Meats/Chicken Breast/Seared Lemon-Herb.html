Chicken Breast: Seared Lemon-Herb

Serves 4. Cut into pieces first for more browning and a 10-minute cook time. Finish with a squeeze of lemon.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 800g / 1.75 lb), cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil, plus more if needed
  • 1 lemon: zest and 2 tbsp (30g) juice
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried oregano
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Toss the chicken pieces with the lemon zest, oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  3. Add the chicken in a single layer (work in two batches if crowded). Sear 4 to 5 minutes per side until golden and cooked to 165°F (74°C), about 8 to 10 minutes total.
  4. Squeeze the lemon juice over the pan, toss 30 seconds, and serve.
