Chicken Breast: Seared Salt-Pepper-Garlic

Serves 4. Nothing but salt, pepper, and garlic — the sear does the talking. Watch the garlic so it browns, not burns.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 800g / 1.75 lb), cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil, plus more if needed
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), thinly sliced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken pieces with the salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic slices and cook 30 seconds until fragrant but not browned; push to the side.
  3. Add the chicken in a single layer. Sear 4 to 5 minutes per side until golden and cooked to 165°F (74°C), about 8 to 10 minutes total.
  4. Toss the garlic through the chicken and serve.
