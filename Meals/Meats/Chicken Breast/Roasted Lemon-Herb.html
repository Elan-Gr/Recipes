Chicken Breast: Roasted Lemon-Herb

Serves 4. Whole breasts roasted hot and fast with lemon, oregano, and garlic. Rest before slicing so the juices stay put.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 800g / 1.75 lb)
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 1 lemon: zest and 2 tbsp (30g) juice
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried oregano
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Pat the chicken dry. Rub all over with the olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  3. Arrange on the pan with space between pieces. Roast 20 to 25 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).
  4. Rest 5 minutes, then slice against the grain and serve.
