Chicken Breast: Seared Smoked Paprika-Garlic

Serves 4. Pieces get a deep reddish crust in minutes — ideal for bowls, wraps, or fried rice.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 800g / 1.75 lb), cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil, plus more if needed
  • 2 tsp (5g) smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (3g) onion powder
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Toss the chicken pieces with the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  3. Sear the chicken in a single layer 4 to 5 minutes per side, until deeply browned and cooked to 165°F (74°C), about 8 to 10 minutes total. Work in batches if needed.
  4. Serve immediately.
