Chicken Breast: Roasted Salt-Pepper-Garlic

Serves 4. The minimalist version: aggressive seasoning, hot oven, juicy chicken. A reliable weekly staple.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 800g / 1.75 lb)
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), minced
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Pat the chicken dry. Rub with olive oil, then the minced garlic, salt, and pepper, pressing the garlic onto the surface.
  3. Roast 20 to 25 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).
  4. Rest 5 minutes, then slice and serve.
