Chicken Breast: Roasted Smoked Paprika-Garlic

Serves 4. Smoky, savory crust from paprika and garlic powder — great sliced over rice or salads.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 800g / 1.75 lb)
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (5g) smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 1 tsp (3g) onion powder
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Pat the chicken dry. Mix the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper; rub the chicken with olive oil, then coat evenly with the spice mix.
  3. Roast 20 to 25 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).
  4. Rest 5 minutes, then slice and serve.
