Chicken Thighs: Pan-Seared, Lemon-Oregano

Serves 4. Stovetop method: skin renders slowly under a lid, then flips to finish. Bright lemon-oregano seasoning.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.2 kg / 2.6 lb)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 lemon: zest and 2 tbsp (30g) juice
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried oregano
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Pat the thighs dry. Rub with the lemon zest, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy skillet over medium-low heat. Add the thighs skin-side down and cover with a lid.
  3. Cook covered 15 minutes without moving, until the skin is deep golden and much of the fat has rendered.
  4. Flip, squeeze the lemon juice over, and cook uncovered 8 to 10 minutes more, until the internal temperature reaches 175°F (80°C).
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve.
