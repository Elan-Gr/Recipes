Chicken Thighs: Braised in Tomato-Herb Sauce

Serves 4. Low-and-slow braise: seared thighs finish in crushed tomatoes until spoon-tender. Serve over rice or with bread.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.2 kg / 2.6 lb)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 large onion (150g), diced
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), minced
  • 800g (28 oz / about 3 1/3 cups) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 240g (1 cup) chicken stock
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried thyme
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Pat the thighs dry and season with the salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat and sear the thighs skin-side down 5 minutes until browned; flip and sear 2 minutes. Remove to a plate.
  3. In the same pot, cook the onion 5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, thyme, and oregano; cook 1 minute.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes and stock, scraping up the browned bits. Return the thighs, skin-side up; the liquid should come about a third of the way up the chicken.
  5. Cover and bake 2 hours, then uncover and bake 30 minutes more, until the meat is tender and nearly falling off the bone.
  6. Skim excess fat if desired and serve.
