Chicken Thighs: Pan-Seared, Garlic-Rosemary

Serves 4. Same covered sear method with a woodsy garlic-rosemary rub. Add the rosemary early so it perfumes the rendering fat.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.2 kg / 2.6 lb)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), minced
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried rosemary, crushed
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Pat the thighs dry. Rub with the garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy skillet over medium-low heat. Add the thighs skin-side down and cover.
  3. Cook covered 15 minutes without moving, until the skin is crisp and golden.
  4. Flip and cook uncovered 8 to 10 minutes more, until the internal temperature reaches 175°F (80°C).
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve.
