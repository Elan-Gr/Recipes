Chicken Thighs: Oven-Roasted Crispy-Skin, Garlic-Herb

Serves 4. Bone-in, skin-on thighs roasted skin-up at high heat, finished under the broiler for shattering skin. Rest before serving.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.2 kg / 2.6 lb)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), minced
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried thyme
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 420°F (215°C). Line a sheet pan with foil and set a wire rack on it if you have one.
  2. Pat the thighs very dry. Rub with the olive oil, then the garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper, getting seasoning under the skin where possible.
  3. Arrange skin-side up with space between pieces. Roast 30 to 35 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 175°F (80°C).
  4. Broil 2 to 3 minutes, watching closely, until the skin is deep golden and crisp.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve.
