Chicken Thighs: Oven-Roasted Crispy-Skin, Smoked Paprika

Serves 4. Smoky-spicy crust, same crisp-skin roast method. The paprika darkens quickly — that color is flavor, not burning.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.2 kg / 2.6 lb)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (5g) smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp (3g) ground cumin
  • 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 420°F (215°C). Line a sheet pan with foil and set a wire rack on it if you have one.
  2. Pat the thighs very dry. Rub with the olive oil, then coat with the paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Arrange skin-side up with space between pieces. Roast 30 to 35 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 175°F (80°C).
  4. Broil 2 to 3 minutes, watching closely, until the skin crisps.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve.
