Chicken Thighs: Braised in Chicken Stock with Onion and Thyme

Serves 4. A lighter braise than the tomato version — the stock reduces into a silky pan sauce.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.2 kg / 2.6 lb)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 2 onions (300g), sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), minced
  • 480g (2 cups) chicken stock
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Pat the thighs dry and season with the salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat and sear the thighs skin-side down 5 minutes; flip and sear 2 minutes. Remove to a plate.
  3. In the same pot, cook the onions 6 to 8 minutes until soft and golden at the edges. Add the garlic and thyme; cook 1 minute.
  4. Add the stock and bay leaves, scraping up the browned bits. Return the thighs skin-side up; liquid should come about a third of the way up.
  5. Cover and bake 2 hours, then uncover and bake 30 minutes more, until tender.
  6. Remove the bay leaves, skim fat if desired, and serve with the pan juices.
