Vegetables: Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Balsamic

Serves 4 as a side. Halved sprouts roast cut-side down for maximum caramelization; balsamic goes on at the end.

Ingredients

  • 500g (about 1 lb 2 oz) Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 1 tbsp (15g) balsamic vinegar
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Toss the sprouts with the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Arrange cut-side down on a rimmed baking sheet.
  3. Roast 20 to 25 minutes, until the cut sides are deep brown and the sprouts are tender.
  4. Drizzle with the balsamic vinegar, toss, and serve.
