Vegetables: Roasted Broccoli with Lemon and Garlic

Serves 4 as a side. High-heat roast with garlic and a squeeze of lemon at the end; the florets char at the edges.

Ingredients

  • 500g (about 1 lb 2 oz / 6 cups) broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 1 lemon: zest and 1 tbsp (15g) juice
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Toss the broccoli with the olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper on a rimmed baking sheet.
  3. Roast 15 to 20 minutes, tossing halfway, until the stems are tender and the florets are charred at the edges.
  4. Toss with the lemon zest and juice and serve.
