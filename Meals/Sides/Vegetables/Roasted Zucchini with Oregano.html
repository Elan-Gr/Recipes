Vegetables: Roasted Zucchini with Oregano

Serves 4 as a side. Cut thick so it roasts instead of steaming; oregano and a hot oven do the rest.

Ingredients

  • 500g (about 2 medium) zucchini, cut into half-moons 1/2 inch thick
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (4g) dried oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss the zucchini with the olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Spread on a rimmed baking sheet with space between pieces.
  3. Roast 18 to 20 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden at the edges and tender.
