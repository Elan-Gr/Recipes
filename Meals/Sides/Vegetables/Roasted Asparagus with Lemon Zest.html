Vegetables: Roasted Asparagus with Lemon Zest

Serves 4 as a side. Ten minutes in a hot oven; lemon zest keeps it bright.

Ingredients

  • 400g (about 14 oz / 1 bunch) asparagus, tough ends trimmed
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 1 lemon: zest only
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss the asparagus with the olive oil, salt, and pepper on a rimmed baking sheet.
  3. Roast 10 to 12 minutes, until crisp-tender and lightly browned in spots.
  4. Toss with the lemon zest and serve.
