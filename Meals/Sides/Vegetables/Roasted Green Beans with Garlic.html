Vegetables: Roasted Green Beans with Garlic

Serves 4 as a side. Roasting gives green beans blistered spots and concentrated flavor — better than steaming.

Ingredients

  • 400g (about 14 oz / 4 cups) green beans, trimmed
  • 1 1/2 tbsp (22g) olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1.5g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Toss the green beans with the olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper on a rimmed baking sheet.
  3. Roast 14 to 16 minutes, tossing halfway, until tender with blistered spots.
