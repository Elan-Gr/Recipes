Vegetables: Sautéed Spinach with Garlic

Serves 4 as a side. A big pile of spinach wilts down in minutes — work in batches so it sautés instead of steaming.

Ingredients

  • 300g (about 10 oz / 10 cups) baby spinach
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp (0.5g) red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes (if using); cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the spinach in large handfuls, tossing as it wilts, 3 to 4 minutes total.
  4. Season with the salt and lemon juice; serve immediately.
