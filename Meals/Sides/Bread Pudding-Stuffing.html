Bread Pudding/Stuffing

From How Sweet Eats. Golden crunchy edges, soft inside. See variations below for a dairy-free version and a bread pudding version.

Ingredients

  • 18–24 oz (510–680 g) bread cubes — 1.5 loaves, about 12–14 cups; preferably toasted or stale. Use more bread when including fewer vegetables: with the full onion/celery amounts, the lower end works; omitting vegetables, use the higher end.
  • 1 cup (227 g) unsalted butter — or 1 cup (218 g) neutral oil for dairy-free
  • 3 cups (~480 g) diced sweet onion, roughly 2 large onions (optional)
  • 2 cups (~240 g) diced celery (optional)
  • 6 garlic cloves (~18 g), minced (optional)
  • Kosher salt and pepper — at least ½–1 teaspoon each, to taste
  • 3 tablespoons (~6 g) chopped fresh sage
  • 3 tablespoons (~11 g) chopped fresh parsley
  • 3 tablespoons (~5 g) chopped fresh rosemary
  • 2½ cups (600 g) chicken or vegetable stock — or 2½ cups (600 g) milk + 120 g sugar for bread pudding (100 g if adding other sweet things)
  • 2 large eggs (~100 g), beaten
  • A mixture of fresh herbs for sprinkling

Bread cubes

Stale or toasty bread works best — three options: cut 1½ lb of bread into cubes, spread in a baking dish, loosely tent with foil, and leave overnight; or toast the cubes at 350°F for ~15 minutes until crouton-like; or buy toasted bread cubes in bags. Any cube size works. A mix of two breads (e.g. sourdough + Italian) gives good texture.

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Brush a 9×13 baking dish (or larger dish / foil roasting pan) with melted butter, oil, or nonstick spray. Put the bread cubes in a large mixing bowl or the baking dish.
  2. Heat the butter (or oil) in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Once melted, stir in the onion, celery, and garlic with a big pinch of salt and pepper — at least ½–1 teaspoon each. Cook until the onions and celery soften, 8–10 minutes.
  3. Stir in the sage, parsley, and rosemary; cook 1 minute more. Stir in 1 cup of the stock.
  4. Pour the onion-celery mixture over the bread cubes and toss well to coat.
  5. In a small bowl or measuring cup, whisk together the remaining 1½ cups stock and the 2 eggs. Pour over the bread cubes and fold until thoroughly combined.
  6. Bake 45–50 minutes, until the internal temperature registers 160°F. Tent with foil if browning too fast.

Variations

  • Dairy-free: use 1 cup (218 g) neutral oil instead of the butter, and vegetable stock instead of chicken stock.
  • Bread pudding: replace the 2½ cups stock with 2½ cups (600 g) milk + 120 g sugar (100 g if adding other sweet things), and omit the onion, celery, garlic, sage, parsley, and rosemary. Keep the butter (or oil) and eggs; bake as above.

To serve 4: halve the recipe exactly; bake in an 8×8 or 9×9 dish, same time. To serve 12–18: double exactly; bake in a 10×15 roasting pan or two 9×13 dishes, same time or ~15 minutes longer. Can be made a day ahead and reheated — take out of the fridge 60 minutes before reheating.

Yield: 8–12 servings
