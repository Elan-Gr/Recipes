# Vegetables

## Broccoli

Broccoli is a nutrient-rich vegetable that is an excellent source of essential vitamins and minerals, including vitamin C, vitamin K, and folate, making it a valuable addition to a balanced diet. Additionally, it is low in calories and a good source of dietary fiber, promoting digestive health and overall well-being.

**Cooking Methods**

- Roast at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for 15-20 minutes

**Seasonings**

- Broccoli absorbs sauces well

## Brussel Sprouts

Brussels sprouts are a nutritious vegetable that provides a rich source of vitamins, particularly vitamin K and vitamin C, and are also high in dietary fiber. Their nutritional profile makes them a great addition to a healthy diet, aiding in immune support and promoting bone health.

**Cooking Methods**

- Roast at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for 15-20 minutes on a sheet pan or in a casserole dish.

## Carrots

Carrots are a nutrient-dense vegetable packed with beta-carotene, which the body converts into vitamin A. They are also a good source of dietary fiber, promoting digestive health, and contain various vitamins and minerals, making them a valuable addition to a balanced diet, supporting both eye health and overall well-being.

**Cooking Methods**

- Coat in oil and roast at 400 degrees for about 20 minutes

**Seasonings**

- Something slightly sweet

## Kale

**Cooking Methods**

- Roast at about 350 degrees Fahrenheit for about 10 minutes
- Sautee in pan
- Rub in olive oil and eat in salad

**Seasonings**

- Citrus, such as lemon and lemon zest

## Spinach

**Cooking Methods**

- Sautee in pan

## Zucchini

**Cooking Methods**

- Roast at 425 degrees for Fahrenheit 15-25 minutes
- Sautee in pan

## Asparagus

**Cooking Methods**

- Roast at 425 degrees Fahrenheit for about 10-15 minutes
- Sautee in a frying pan

## Green Beans

**Cooking Methods**

- Steam
- Roast at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for 12-18 minutes
- Sautee in pan

## Bell peppers

**Cooking Methods**

- Sautee in pan
- Roast in oven at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for about 10-15 minutes

## Cauliflower

**Cooking Methods**

- Roast at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for 15-20 minutes
