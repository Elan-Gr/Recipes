Potatoes: Breakfast Potatoes

Serves 4 as a side. A quick microwave par-cook means the insides are creamy by the time the outsides crisp in the pan.

Ingredients

  • 750g (1.65 lb) Yukon Gold or red potatoes, diced into 3/4-inch pieces
  • 2 tbsp (30g) olive oil
  • 1 tsp (3g) smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp (3g) onion powder
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Put the diced potatoes in a microwave-safe bowl, cover, and microwave 4 minutes to partially cook the insides. Drain any liquid.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over high heat until shimmering.
  3. Add the potatoes in a single layer and cook undisturbed 3 to 4 minutes until browned, then toss and cook 6 to 8 minutes more, stirring occasionally, until crisp all over.
  4. Toss with the paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper; cook 1 minute more and serve.
