Potatoes: Roasted Yukon Golds with Garlic and Thyme

Serves 4 as a side. Yukon Golds roast up creamy inside and crisp outside; garlic goes in for the second half so it does not burn.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (2.2 lb) Yukon Gold potatoes, quartered
  • 3 tbsp (45g) olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves (12g), minced
  • 2 tsp (4g) fresh thyme leaves (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a rimmed baking sheet with space between pieces.
  3. Roast 20 minutes, then toss with the garlic and thyme.
  4. Roast 10 to 15 minutes more, until golden and crisp at the edges.
