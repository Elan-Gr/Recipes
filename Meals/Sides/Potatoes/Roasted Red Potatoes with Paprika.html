Potatoes: Roasted Red Potatoes with Paprika

Serves 4 as a side. Waxy reds hold their shape; paprika gives a smoky red crust.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (2.2 lb) red potatoes, halved (quartered if large)
  • 3 tbsp (45g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (5g) smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread cut-side down on a rimmed baking sheet with space between pieces.
  4. Roast 25 to 30 minutes, until the cut sides are deep golden and crisp and the potatoes are tender.
