Potatoes: Crispy Roasted Russets

Serves 4 as a side. Parboiling first is the trick: rough, starchy edges turn shatteringly crisp in a hot oven.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (2.2 lb, about 3 large) russet potatoes, cut into 1 1/2-inch chunks
  • 3 tbsp (45g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper
  • 1 tsp (2g) dried rosemary, crushed

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Put a large rimmed baking sheet in the oven to preheat.
  2. Boil the potato chunks in well-salted water 7 to 8 minutes, until the edges look soft and you worry they might fall apart. Drain well and let steam-dry 2 minutes.
  3. Toss the potatoes in a bowl with the olive oil, salt, pepper, and rosemary, roughing up the edges slightly.
  4. Spread on the hot baking sheet with space between pieces. Roast 35 to 40 minutes, flipping halfway, until deep golden and crisp.
