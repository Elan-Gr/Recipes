Potatoes: Baked Russets

Serves 4 as a side. Salty, oiled skin and fluffy interior — the simplest potato there is.

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes (about 900g / 2 lb total)
  • 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Scrub the potatoes and dry thoroughly. Rub with the olive oil and salt.
  3. Place directly on the oven rack and bake about 60 minutes, until the skin is crisp and a knife slides in easily.
  4. Cut a cross in the top, squeeze the ends to fluff, and serve.
