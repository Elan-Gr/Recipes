Potatoes: Mashed Yukon Golds with Olive Oil

Serves 4 as a side. The parent technique bakes the potatoes whole, then mashes — olive oil instead of butter keeps it pareve.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (2.2 lb) Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 4 tbsp (60g) extra-virgin olive oil, plus more to serve
  • 120g (1/2 cup) warm water
  • 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt
  • 1 tsp (3g) black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Halve the potatoes, rub the cut sides lightly with 1 tbsp of the olive oil, and place cut-side down on a baking sheet.
  3. Bake about 60 minutes, until completely tender when pierced.
  4. Scoop the flesh into a bowl (discard skins or save for another use). Mash with the remaining 3 tbsp olive oil, the warm water, salt, and pepper until smooth but still a little rustic.
  5. Serve with an extra drizzle of olive oil.
