# French Onion Soup

Make onion soup with soup mix or from scratch. Caramelize onions. Put onions in bowl and put a piece of French bread on top of the onions. Pour soup over the bread and onions. Put a slice of Swiss cheese on top of the bread. Either broil the soup to melt and brown the cheese, or melt cheese in microwave and brown with a blow torch.
