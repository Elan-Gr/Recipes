French Onion Soup

Ingredients

  • onion soup (made with soup mix or from scratch)
  • onions
  • French bread
  • Swiss cheese

Instructions

  1. Caramelize onions.
  2. Put onions in bowl and put a piece of French bread on top of the onions.
  3. Pour soup over the bread and onions.
  4. Put a slice of Swiss cheese on top of the bread.
  5. Either broil the soup to melt and brown the cheese, or melt cheese in microwave and brown with a blow torch.
