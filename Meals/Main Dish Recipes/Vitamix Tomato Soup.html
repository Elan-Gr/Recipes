Vitamix Tomato Soup

From Cucina by Elena — a tomato basil soup, light and creamy: stew the tomatoes and aromatics first, then blend smooth and finish with cream. Works with canned tomatoes year-round. (The post mentions olive oil in passing, but the recipe card lists none — none included.)

Ingredients

  • 1 (28-ounce) can whole peeled tomatoes (794 g), with their juices
  • ½ cup (120 g) low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth, or water
  • 1 tablespoon (~10 g) coarsely chopped shallot
  • ½ garlic clove (~1.5 g)
  • 5 leaves fresh basil (~2.5 g)
  • ½ tablespoon (~8 g) tomato paste
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • ¼–½ cup (60–120 g) heavy cream, to taste
  • Freshly grated parmigiano cheese, for serving (~10 g)

Instructions

  1. Place the tomatoes (with their juices), broth, shallot, garlic, basil, and tomato paste in a pot. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook 10–15 minutes, until the shallot is soft and the flavors have melded.
  3. Blend until completely smooth (in batches if needed). Immersion blender: blend right in the pot until silky.
  4. Stir in the heavy cream to taste and warm through gently — don't boil.
  5. Serve hot with freshly grated parmigiano and crusty bread. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.

No blender at all?

  • Finely chop the ingredients, stew on the stove, then push through a sieve for a smooth texture (peel the tomatoes first if going this route).

Variations

  • Spicy: add chili pepper flakes.
  • Deluxe: caramelized or sautéed onions, sun-dried tomatoes, or extra minced garlic.
  • Dairy-free: cashew cream or non-dairy milk instead of heavy cream (the soup is already creamy without it).
  • Kid-friendly: serve with a grilled cheese for dipping.
  • Fresh tomatoes: steam or boil and peel them, or roast halved at 400°F for 15 minutes with a drizzle of olive oil.

Storage: airtight container, up to 3 days. Reheat in the microwave or on the stovetop, stirring occasionally.

Pairs with: oven baked grilled cheese.
