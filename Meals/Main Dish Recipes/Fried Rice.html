Fried Rice

Ingredients

  • day-old rice
  • oil
  • soy sauce
  • spices
  • egg
  • meat

Instructions

  1. Use day-old rice (cooking it in a rice cooker is possible). Make sure pan is super hot and use a good amount of oil.
  2. Fry rice and add soy sauce, spices, egg, meat, ect.

Variations

  • Fried Rice: Chicken and Egg with Peas and Bell Pepper
  • Fried Rice: Beef and Egg with Snap Peas
  • Fried Rice: Tofu and Egg with Edamame
  • Fried Rice: Vegetable and Egg
  • Fried Rice: Chicken and Mushroom
  • Fried Rice: Beef and Broccoli
