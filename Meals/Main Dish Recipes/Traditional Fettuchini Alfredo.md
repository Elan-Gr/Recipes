## Traditional Method

Use 1 lb (454 g) pasta per 1/2 lb (226 g) room temperature butter per 1/2 (around) lb (226 g) grated room temperature parmesan cheese (not shredded, must be as finely grated as possible).

Cook pasta in as little water as possible so the starch is concentrated.

Put butter in a large bowl. Once pasta is cooked, transfer to bowl using tongs, letting some of the water stick. Mix until melted. Slowly add grated parmesan cheese, mixing vigorously to emulsify and moving quickly so that the pasta doesn't have time to cool down. You may not need all the cheese, but probably use most of it. If needed, add a few tablespoons of pasta water to loosen the sauce.

For just one serving, use 3 tablespoons butter, 42 grams parmesan, and 84 grams dried pasta.

Alternatively, you can throw the ingredients in a tupperware and shake vigorously to emulsify.

## American Method

Ingredients

- 1 lb fettuccine
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- Pinch grated nutmeg
- 1 cup (80 grams) grated Parmigiano Reggiano cheese

Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the fettuccine and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. Reserve about 1 cup of pasta water, then drain the fettuccine.

2. In the same pot (no need to rinse), melt the butter over medium-low heat. Stir in the heavy cream, salt, pepper, and nutmeg and bring to a gentle simmer. Let it simmer for about 2 minutes to thicken slightly. Gradually stir in the cheese until melted. For this sauce, you don't need a roux because the fat in the cream provides enough stability.

3. Add the drained fettuccine to the sauce, tossing everything together with tongs until the cheese is melted and the pasta is well coated and warmed through. Gradually add the reserved pasta water a little at a time to achieve your desired sauce consistency. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper, then serve. The sauce will continue to thicken as it sits, so stir in more pasta water as needed. If the sauce becomes too thin, cook over low heat, tossing frequently, until thickened.
