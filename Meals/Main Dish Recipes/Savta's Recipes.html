Chicken Soup

Boil chicken wings, some other parts of chicken, and onion. I add if I can turkey wing. Add water and boil. Clear the foam. Add celery, carrots, zucchini, parsley and roots. Dill. Salt pepper. Bay leaves. Pepper corns. One table spoon of Osem soup and teaspoon of Hawaij. Boil again. Reduce heat and simmer for about an hour.

Separate soup from vegetables and chicken.

You can freeze the soup.

Enjoy. ❤️

Beef Brisket

Sliced onion on the bottom of the sheet. Put brisket on top. Put about 5/6 garlic cloves.

Hints hot sauce (one bottle) in the corners. Or Chilly sauce

1/2 cup of water.

3 Bay leaves.

10 black pepper. Sprinkle over.

2 Tablespoons onion soup powder. Sprinkle over.

Cover tight with foil paper.

Bake on 350 for about 3 hours.

Let it cool.

Cut and before serving put again in covered dish in the oven on 300 for an hour.

After cooking you can freeze it and cut it 1/2 defrosted. It is much easier to cut then.

Chicken in the oven - from Nanny

Best with dark meat legs and wings.

Mix:

  • 1/2 cup orange juice or any fruit juice
  • 4 Tbs. olive oil
  • 2 Tbs. Honey
  • 1/2 cup white wine
  • 2 Ts. mustard
  • 2 Ts onion soup powder
  • Salt pepper garlic
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce or Teriyaki

Arrange chicken in dish.

Pour mixture on it. Cover with foil.

Bake for about 2 hours. Remove cover and bake for another 30 min.
