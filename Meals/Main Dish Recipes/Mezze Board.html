Instructions

  1. Turmeric Rice: rice with turmeric for color and olive oil baked in a covered sheet ban.
  2. Cherry Tomato Salad: cut cherry tomatoes in half, mix with mozzarella pearls, salt, and pepper.
  3. Carrot salad: shredded carrots with golden raisins, olive oil, herbs.
  4. Labneh: strain full fat Greek yogurt with cheese cloth.
  5. Serve with Kalamata olives, hummus, roasted tomatoes, roasted eggplant, baked salmon side, falafel, cabbage slaw, and pickled onions.
