Chili

Ingredients

  • onions
  • 1 pound ground beef or turkey
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • chili powder
  • other spices
  • dried chilies (optional)
  • water

Instructions

  1. Sweat onions in saucepan.
  2. Add a pound of ground beef or turkey, and brown the meat.
  3. Add a can of diced tomatoes, chili powder, and any other spices.
  4. If using dried chilies, toast the chilies in a pan, then blend them with some water into a sauce. Add that to the chili.
  5. Let simmer for about a half an hour.
