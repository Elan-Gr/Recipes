Fried Rice: Vegetable and Egg

Serves 4. Egg-only fried rice loaded with vegetables — scallions, peas, and bell pepper. Vegetarian.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (740g) cooked day-old long-grain white rice (from about 1 1/2 cups / 280g dry rice)
  • 4 large eggs (about 200g), beaten
  • 100g (2/3 cup) frozen peas, thawed
  • 1 bell pepper (about 150g), diced
  • 60g (about 6) scallions, thinly sliced, whites and greens separated
  • 3 tbsp (45g) neutral oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp (45g) soy sauce
  • 1 tsp (5g) toasted sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 1/2 tsp (1g) white pepper
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Scramble the eggs 60 to 90 seconds into soft ribbons; remove to a plate.
  2. Add the remaining 2 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the scallion whites, bell pepper, and garlic 2 to 3 minutes; add the peas for the last minute.
  3. Add the rice and fry 3 to 4 minutes, breaking up clumps, until hot and lightly crisped.
  4. Drizzle the soy sauce around the pan edge and toss.
  5. Return the eggs. Add the sesame oil, white pepper, salt, and scallion greens; toss 1 minute and serve.
