Fried Rice: Chicken and Mushroom

Serves 4. Chicken thighs stay juicy; mushrooms soak up the soy and sesame. Let the mushrooms brown undisturbed for real flavor.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (740g) cooked day-old long-grain white rice (from about 1 1/2 cups / 280g dry rice)
  • 300g (about 10 oz) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, diced into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 250g (about 3 cups) mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 large eggs (about 100g), beaten
  • 3 tbsp (45g) neutral oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp (45g) soy sauce
  • 1 tsp (5g) toasted sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 4 scallions (60g), thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp (1g) white pepper
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Season the diced chicken with 1/2 tsp of the salt.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Sear the chicken 5 minutes until cooked through; remove to a plate.
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil and the mushrooms in a single layer; cook undisturbed 3 minutes, then stir and cook 2 minutes more until browned. Remove to the plate.
  4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil and scramble the eggs 60 seconds; push to the side.
  5. Add the garlic and rice; fry 3 to 4 minutes, breaking up clumps.
  6. Drizzle the soy sauce around the pan edge and toss.
  7. Return the chicken and mushrooms. Add the sesame oil, white pepper, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and scallions; toss 1 minute and serve.
