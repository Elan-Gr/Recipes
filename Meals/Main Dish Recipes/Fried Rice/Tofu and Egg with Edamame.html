Fried Rice: Tofu and Egg with Edamame

Serves 4. Pressed, well-dried tofu crisps up nicely; edamame brings protein and bite. Vegetarian.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (740g) cooked day-old long-grain white rice (from about 1 1/2 cups / 280g dry rice)
  • 400g (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed 15 minutes, cut into 3/4-inch cubes and patted dry
  • 3 large eggs (about 150g), beaten
  • 150g (1 cup) shelled edamame, thawed if frozen
  • 3 tbsp (45g) neutral oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp (45g) soy sauce
  • 1 tsp (5g) toasted sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 4 scallions (60g), thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp (1g) white pepper
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Season the tofu cubes with 1/2 tsp of the salt.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Sear the tofu 5 to 6 minutes, turning, until golden on most sides; remove to a plate.
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil and scramble the eggs 60 to 90 seconds; remove to the plate.
  4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the edamame and garlic 2 minutes.
  5. Add the rice and fry 3 to 4 minutes, breaking up clumps.
  6. Drizzle the soy sauce around the pan edge and toss.
  7. Return the tofu and eggs. Add the sesame oil, white pepper, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and scallions; toss 1 minute and serve.
