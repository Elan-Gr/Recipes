Fried Rice: Chicken and Egg with Peas and Bell Pepper

Serves 4. The classic: day-old rice, seared chicken, ribboned egg, sweet peas and bell pepper. Keep the wok or large skillet screaming hot.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (740g) cooked day-old long-grain white rice (from about 1 1/2 cups / 280g dry rice)
  • 300g (about 10 oz) boneless, skinless chicken breast, diced into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 3 large eggs (about 150g), beaten
  • 150g (1 cup) frozen peas, thawed
  • 1 bell pepper (about 150g), diced
  • 3 tbsp (45g) neutral oil (such as avocado or canola), divided
  • 3 tbsp (45g) soy sauce
  • 1 tsp (5g) toasted sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 4 scallions (60g), thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp (1g) white pepper
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Season the diced chicken with 1/2 tsp of the salt.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a large wok or 12-inch skillet over high heat until shimmering. Sear the chicken 4 to 5 minutes until cooked through; remove to a plate.
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil to the pan. Pour in the beaten eggs and scramble 60 to 90 seconds into soft ribbons; remove to the plate.
  4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the bell pepper and garlic 2 minutes, then add the peas and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Add the rice, breaking up any clumps. Fry 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until hot and lightly crisped in spots.
  6. Drizzle the soy sauce around the edge of the pan so it sizzles, then toss everything together.
  7. Return the chicken and eggs to the pan. Add the sesame oil, white pepper, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and scallions; toss 1 minute and serve.
