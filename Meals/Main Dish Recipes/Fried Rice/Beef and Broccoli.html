Fried Rice: Beef and Broccoli

Serves 4. Takeout flavors, wok hei at home: seared beef strips and crisp-tender broccoli over smoky fried rice.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (740g) cooked day-old long-grain white rice (from about 1 1/2 cups / 280g dry rice)
  • 300g (about 10 oz) sirloin or flank steak, cut into thin strips
  • 250g (about 3 cups) broccoli florets, cut small
  • 2 large eggs (about 100g), beaten
  • 3 tbsp (45g) neutral oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp (45g) soy sauce
  • 1 tsp (5g) toasted sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 4 scallions (60g), thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp (1g) white pepper
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Season the beef strips with 1/2 tsp of the salt.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Sear the beef 2 to 3 minutes; remove to a plate.
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil and the broccoli with a splash (2 tbsp / 30g) of water; cover 2 minutes to steam, then uncover and stir-fry 1 minute. Remove to the plate.
  4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil and scramble the eggs 60 seconds.
  5. Add the garlic and rice; fry 3 to 4 minutes, breaking up clumps.
  6. Drizzle the soy sauce around the pan edge and toss.
  7. Return the beef and broccoli. Add the sesame oil, white pepper, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and scallions; toss 1 minute and serve.
