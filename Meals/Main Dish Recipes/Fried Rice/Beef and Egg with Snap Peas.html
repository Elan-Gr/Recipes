Fried Rice: Beef and Egg with Snap Peas

Serves 4. Thin strips of sirloin sear fast and stay tender; snap peas add crunch.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (740g) cooked day-old long-grain white rice (from about 1 1/2 cups / 280g dry rice)
  • 300g (about 10 oz) sirloin steak, cut into thin strips
  • 3 large eggs (about 150g), beaten
  • 200g (about 3 cups) snap peas, halved on the diagonal
  • 3 tbsp (45g) neutral oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp (45g) soy sauce
  • 1 tsp (5g) toasted sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
  • 1 tbsp (15g) minced fresh ginger
  • 4 scallions (60g), thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp (1g) white pepper
  • 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Season the beef strips with 1/2 tsp of the salt.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Sear the beef 2 to 3 minutes until browned but still pink inside; remove to a plate.
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil and scramble the eggs 60 to 90 seconds; remove to the plate.
  4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the snap peas, garlic, and ginger 2 minutes until bright green.
  5. Add the rice and fry 3 to 4 minutes, breaking up clumps.
  6. Drizzle the soy sauce around the pan edge, toss, then return the beef and eggs.
  7. Add the sesame oil, white pepper, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and scallions; toss 1 minute and serve.
