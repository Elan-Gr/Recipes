## Mac and Cheese Sauce #1

Ingredients:

- 75 grams of American Cheese
- 75 grams of another hard cheese (such as cheddar, Gruyère, Monterey Jack, or Swiss)
- 1 cups of milk
- A pinch of salt
- 1 tablespoon of butter
- Any other seasonings you would like to include (such as garlic powder, smoked paprika, hot sauce, ect.)

Directions:

1. Warm up the milk in a saucepan on medium heat
2. Slowly add the American cheese, followed by the other cheeses and spices, and whisk until melted.
3. Remove from heat and stir in butter.

This recipe makes a loose sauce that is good for baked mac and cheese, but you can add more cheese to make it thicker. Use 3 or for cups of milk per pound of pasta.

## Mac and Cheese Sauce #2

Ingredients

- 150 grams of one or more hard cheese of your choosing (such as cheddar, Gruyère, Monterey Jack, or Swiss) (about 1.5 cups). Use a good quality cheese because cheap cheese will make the sauce taste bland.
- 1 cup (240 grams) of milk (Make some of it cream for richer sauce)
- 1 Tablespoon (x grams) of flour
- A pinch of salt
- Any other seasonings you would like to include (such as garlic powder, smoked paprika, hot sauce, ect.)

Directions:

1. Melt butter on medium heat
2. Whisk in flour and stir until mixture is browned. Roux should cook for at least 3 minutes.
3. Whisk in milk and seasonings
4. Let sauce cup up to a simmer
5. Once sauce has come up to a simmer, take off heat and slowly whisk in cheese

(note: Ensure that your pot is narrow enough that your cheese is slowly melting in the milk and not just melting on the bottom of the pot)
