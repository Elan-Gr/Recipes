## Breakfast

**Baked French toast**

See baked French toast recipe

**Scrambled eggs**

See scrambled eggs recipe, but blend cold butter and corn starch into egg and then cook slowly in large pan

**Sheet Pan Omelets**

Line baking sheet with parchment paper, and grease parchment paper. Whisk around 10 eggs per baking sheet, as well as the toppings. Pour over baking sheet and spread out. Bake at 350 degrees for around 15 minutes.

## Main Courses

**Mac and cheese**

See mac and cheese recipe

Can shred cheese ahead of time

**Fish Taco Bar**

Cook fish and all toppings such as peppers, onions, ect. Can cook ahead of time and reheat in aluminum pans. Serve with tortillas, avocado, rice/beans,

**Chicken thighs**

Marinate chicken thighs and load up on baking sheet. Bake using provided recipe.

**Chicken wings**

See chicken wings recipe

**Pizza**

See pizza recipe. Don't try to double the batch. Make each batch in it's own bowl otherwise it will be difficult to knead and will not rise properly.
