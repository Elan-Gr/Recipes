# Granola Recipe (Metric / Grams)

## Ingredients

- 360 g old-fashioned rolled oats (use certified gluten-free oats if needed)
- 180 g raw nuts and/or seeds (example: 120 g pecans + 60 g pepitas)
- 5 g fine-grain sea salt (or 4 g table salt)
- 1.3 g ground cinnamon
- 110 g coconut oil or olive oil, melted
- 160 g honey
- 4 g vanilla extract
- 100 g dried fruit (e.g., dried cranberries), chopped if large

**Optional mix-ins (add after baking):**

- 85 g chocolate chips **or**
- 40 g coconut flakes

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to **175°C (350°F)**. Line a large, rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the oats, nuts and/or seeds, salt, and cinnamon. Stir to blend.
3. Add the oil, maple syrup or honey, and vanilla. Mix well until all oats and nuts are evenly coated.
4. Spread the mixture evenly onto the prepared baking sheet.
5. Bake for **21–24 minutes**, stirring halfway through. For extra-clumpy granola, press the granola down firmly after stirring.
6. Remove from oven when lightly golden. The granola will continue to crisp as it cools.
7. Let cool completely, undisturbed (at least **45 minutes**).
8. Once cooled, add dried fruit and optional chocolate chips or coconut flakes. Break into chunks if desired.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container at room temperature for **1–2 weeks**, or freeze for up to **3 months**. If frozen, allow granola to warm to room temperature before serving.
