Oat Milk Chia Pudding

Tasty vegan oat milk chia pudding; healthy meal prep breakfast made with just 3 pantry staple ingredients.

Prep Time: 5 mins | Time in the fridge: 2 hrs | Total Time: 2 hrs 5 mins. Course: Breakfast, Snack | Cuisine: American, British | Diet: Vegan, Vegetarian | Servings: 2 servings | Calories: 196 kcal | Author: Claire

Equipment

Whisk

Ingredients

  • 42.5 g chia seeds
  • 236.59 ml oat milk (certified gluten free, if required)
  • 0.99 tbsp maple syrup or agave nectar

Optional add-ins

  • 0.25 tsp cinnamon or cardamom
  • 0.25 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch brown sugar

Instructions

  1. Combine the chia seeds, oat milk and maple syrup (or agave nectar) in a mason jar or bowl.
  2. Whisk well to distribute the chia seeds through the milk and set aside in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  3. If you can, give the chia pudding a whisk after about an hour to help break up any clumps of chia seeds that form.
  4. Add any toppings you like and enjoy!

Notes

Adjust the amount of maple syrup to taste preference. Using a whisk rather than a spoon is better for breaking up the chia seeds — they do like to form clumps! Giving the chia pudding a whisk after an hour or so helps to evenly distribute the chia seeds amongst the milk. For a looser chia pudding, add a touch more oat milk.
