# Quiche

## Ingredients

- 5 large eggs
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) milk
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 cup (about 120 grams) shredded cheese (e.g., Swiss, cheddar, or Gruyère)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
   - a. If using a store-bought pie crust, follow the package instructions for pre-baking (often called "blind baking"). This usually involves pricking the crust with a fork and baking it for a short time to prevent it from becoming soggy.
   - b. If making your own pie crust, roll it out and fit it into a pie dish. Prick the bottom with a fork and bake it for about 10–15 minutes until it's lightly golden. Allow it to cool.
2. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, and heavy cream. Season the mixture with salt and pepper to taste.
3. Sprinkle your choice of shredded cheese onto the pre-baked pie crust. If you're adding other ingredients like vegetables, scatter them evenly over the cheese. Make sure the vegetables are cooked, because otherwise they will release a lot of water into the egg.
4. Carefully pour the egg and cream mixture over the cheese and other ingredients in the pie crust.
5. Place the quiche in the preheated oven and bake for 30–45 minutes or until the quiche is set and the top is lightly browned. You can check for doneness by inserting a knife or toothpick into the center; it should come out clean when the quiche is ready.

Once done, remove the quiche from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
