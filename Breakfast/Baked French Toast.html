Baked French Toast

Ingredients

  • Bread, sliced thickly
  • Eggs
  • Cream or milk
  • Vanilla
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Slice bread thickly.
  2. Soak in custard mixture consisting of eggs, cream/milk, vanilla, salt, sugar, cinnamon, etc.
  3. Bake at 350 degrees for 15–20 minutes on parchment paper.
  4. Flip halfway through.
  5. Finish under the broiler to brown.
