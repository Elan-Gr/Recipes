German Pancakes ✓ tried

From: German Pancakes.pdf

Golden pan-style egg dish, with lots of big bubbles while baking. Serve with maple syrup. Submitted by BRENNASPITZ.

Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 30 mins | Total Time: 45 mins | Servings: 6

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup butter
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 6 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • ⅛ teaspoon salt

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Melt butter in a medium baking dish.
  3. Mix flour, milk, eggs, and salt in a medium bowl.
  4. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish.
  5. Bake on center rack in the preheated oven for 30 to 40 minutes, until golden brown.

Nutrition Facts

Per serving: 236 calories; total fat 14g; saturated fat 7g; cholesterol 210mg; sodium 190mg; total carbohydrate 18g; dietary fiber 1g; total sugars 2g; protein 10g; calcium 80mg; iron 2mg; potassium 153mg.
