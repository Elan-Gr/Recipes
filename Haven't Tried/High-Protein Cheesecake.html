High-Protein Cheesecake

Surprisingly tastes like cheesecake, took no time at all to prep.

Source: Instagram reel

Ingredients

For 1 generous serving

  • Plain Greek yogurt (high-protein / 0%) — 1 cup (approx. 225g)
  • Large egg — 1
  • Honey — 2 tbsp (approx. 30g)
  • Vanilla extract — 1/2 tsp
  • Salt — a tiny pinch

Method

  1. Mix everything really well together in a bowl until completely smooth.
  2. Pour the mixture into a lightly greased small ramekin or oven-safe dish.
  3. Bake at 325°F (approx. 165°C) for 20 minutes. The edges should be set while the center still has a slight jiggle.
  4. Let it cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 2 hours (overnight is even better) to firm up and get that cold, creamy cheesecake texture.
  5. Top with fresh berries and enjoy!
