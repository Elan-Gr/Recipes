No Churn Ice Cream

No ice cream maker needed — just whip, mix, and freeze.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla
  • A splash of any liqueur or liquor you have at home

Instructions

  1. Whip the cream to the whipped cream stage.
  2. Add the condensed milk, vanilla, and splash of liqueur or liquor. Mix it up.
  3. Freeze it. Enjoy in a few hours!

The splash of alcohol keeps it from forming crystals and helps the cream remain scoopable right out of the freezer.
