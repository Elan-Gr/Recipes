Pumpkin Brownies

Adapted from Lil' Luna — the original swirls chocolate and pumpkin batters; this version skips the chocolate and mixes the pumpkin through the whole batter. Use canned pumpkin puree, not pie filling.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup (90 g) all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon (2 g) baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon (3 g) salt
  • ¾ cup (170 g) butter, melted — or ¾ cup (163 g) neutral oil for dairy-free
  • 1½ cups (300 g) sugar
  • 2 teaspoons (8 g) vanilla extract
  • 3 eggs (~150 g)
  • ½ cup (122 g) pumpkin puree
  • ½ cup (60 g) chopped walnuts (optional)
  • ¾ teaspoon (2 g) cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon (1 g) ground cloves
  • ½ teaspoon (1 g) ground nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease an 8×8-inch baking pan.
  2. In a bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In a separate bowl, stir together the melted butter (or oil), sugar, and vanilla. Using a spoon, mix in the eggs one at a time. Gradually stir in the flour mixture.
  4. Stir in the pumpkin puree, walnuts, cinnamon, cloves, and nutmeg.
  5. Spread the batter in the pan and bake 45–48 minutes, until the center doesn't look wet or jiggle.
  6. Cool completely before cutting for cleaner slices.

Spice shortcut: replace the cinnamon, cloves, and nutmeg with 1¾ teaspoons pumpkin pie spice. Storage: airtight at room temperature up to 3 days, fridge up to 1 week; freeze up to 6 months.
