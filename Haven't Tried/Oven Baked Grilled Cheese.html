Oven Baked Grilled Cheese

From Natasha's Kitchen — baked on a sheet pan at 400°F, so a whole batch comes out at once with no standing over the stove. Same buttery, crispy toast and melty cheese pull as a pan-fried grilled cheese.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons (43 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 8 slices sandwich bread (about 225 g)
  • 8 thick slices medium cheddar cheese (about 200 g)
  • 4 slices mozzarella cheese (about 85 g)

Yield: 4 sandwiches

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Spread the softened butter on one side of 4 bread slices (about 1 teaspoon per slice) and place them butter-side-down on a rimmed baking sheet. Reader tip: preheat the baking sheet for a crisper crust.
  2. Top each slice with a slice of cheddar, a slice of mozzarella, then another slice of cheddar. Butter one side of the remaining 4 slices and place them butter-side-up on top of the cheese.
  3. Bake 10 minutes, flipping halfway through, until both sides are golden brown. Don't let the sandwiches touch on the sheet pan — scale up or down as needed.

Variations

  • Bread: swap white for rye, whole wheat, sourdough, or gluten-free.
  • Mayonnaise in place of butter works here.
  • Cheese: sharp or mild cheddar, American, Swiss, Gruyère, Monterey Jack, or a combination.
  • Proteins: add a slice of ham, pepperoni, prosciutto, cooked bacon, or pulled pork.
  • Fun additions: potato chips, cut jalapeños, thin-sliced apples or avocado, pesto, tomato, or Brie with plum jam.

Make ahead and storage: let cool completely before storing to keep the crisp. Refrigerate wrapped in parchment or a paper towel in an airtight container, 3–5 days; freeze up to 2 months and thaw overnight in the fridge. Reheat on a sheet pan in the oven until melty (165°F).

Serve with: tomato soup.
