Better Than Bouillon Gravy

From Two Kooks in the Kitchen — 5 minutes, no drippings needed. Pick any Better Than Bouillon base (roasted chicken, roasted beef, seasoned vegetable, mushroom, turkey, etc.). Pan drippings can replace the oil/butter if you have them.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons (41 g) oil or unsalted butter (or half of each)
  • Optional: 3 tablespoons (~18 g) chopped green onions or shallots
  • 3½ tablespoons (26 g) all-purpose flour (4 tbsp for thicker gravy)
  • 1 tablespoon (~18 g) Better Than Bouillon base, any kind (or more to taste)
  • 2 cups (473 g) water

Optional flavor enhancers: salt, black pepper, thyme, poultry seasoning, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, fresh herbs; 1 teaspoon soy sauce, sherry, or Worcestershire sauce; a splash of cream.

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter or heat the oil (or half of each) in a small saucepan over medium heat. Optional: add the green onions and cook 2 minutes.
  2. Stir in the flour and the Better Than Bouillon base. Cook, stirring constantly, 2 minutes, until the roux just starts to turn very light brown — this cooks off the raw flour taste. Don't let it darken or burn. (It should be a loose paste; if too dry, add a bit more oil.)
  3. Whisk in 1 cup water until smooth, then whisk in the remaining 1 cup water. Cook about 3 minutes until thickened. Taste and add optional seasonings as needed. Gravy thickens a bit more on standing.

Cornstarch alternative: skip the flour — stir the base into 2 cups simmering water, then whisk in a slurry of 2 tablespoons cornstarch + 2 tablespoons cold water; simmer 1–2 minutes. Troubleshooting: lumpy → whisk hard, immersion-blend, or strain; too thin → cornstarch slurry (1 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water); too thick → more water; too pale → ½ tsp soy or Worcestershire sauce.

Make ahead: keeps 2–3 days refrigerated, 4 months frozen. Rewarm on medium-low or in the microwave, whisking smooth; thin with water if needed.

Yield: about 2 cups (5–6 servings)
