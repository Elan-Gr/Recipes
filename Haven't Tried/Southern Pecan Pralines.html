Southern Pecan Pralines untried

From House of Nash Eats (site credits "Adapted from AllRecipes") — classic Southern pralines: buttery, caramel-chewy candy studded with pecan halves, cooked to soft-ball stage.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups (~300 g) granulated sugar
  • 1½ cups (~330 g) light brown sugar, packed
  • ¼ tsp (~1.5 g) salt
  • 1 cup (~252 g) evaporated milk (or half-and-half)
  • 6 Tbsp (~85 g) salted butter
  • 1 tsp (~4 g) vanilla extract
  • 2 cups (~200 g) pecan halves

Instructions

  1. Combine the granulated sugar, brown sugar, salt, evaporated milk, and butter in a heavy pot over medium heat. Stir until dissolved and boiling.
  2. Cook to 235–240°F (soft ball stage).
  3. Remove from heat and cool 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the vanilla and pecans, beating about 2 minutes, until the candy loses its gloss and thickens slightly.
  5. Drop heaping tablespoons onto a parchment-lined baking sheet. If the candy seizes, stir in a teaspoon of hot water.
  6. Cool completely.

Store airtight on the counter up to 2 weeks, or freeze up to 2 months.
