# Buttered Biscuits

Source: King Arthur Baking — <https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/buttered-biscuits-recipe>

The easiest way to make tender, buttered biscuits: rather than cutting butter into flour, simply stir cream into the flour and dry ingredients. Pat into a round, cut, bake, and enjoy (with an additional pat of butter on top, of course!).

**Prep:** 15 mins · **Bake:** 20 mins · **Total:** 1 hr 5 mins · **Yield:** 9 to 11 biscuits, depending on size

## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups (210g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1/4 cup (28g) cornstarch
- 3/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
- 1 to 1 1/4 cups (227g to 283g) heavy cream, enough to make a cohesive dough*
- 2 to 3 tablespoons (28g to 42g) butter, melted

\* See "tips," below.

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the flour, cornstarch, salt, baking powder, and sugar. Stir in enough heavy cream to moisten the dough thoroughly. You'll probably use about 1 cup (227g) in the summer, 1 1/4 cups (284g) in the winter, and 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons (256g) at the turn of the seasons. You want to be able to gather the dough together, squeeze it, and have it hang together easily, without dry bits falling off.
2. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured work surface and very gently pat it into an 8" circle about 3/4" thick. (If uncertain about making a freeform 8" round, pat the dough into a lightly floured 8" round cake pan, then turn it out onto a lightly floured surface.)
3. Use a sharp 2 1/4" biscuit cutter to cut rounds. (Larger biscuits may need to bake a bit longer; smaller ones a bit less.) Place on a lightly greased or parchment-lined baking sheet.
4. Brush the biscuits with about half of the melted butter (adds extra flavor).
5. Place the pan in the freezer for 30 minutes. This improves texture and rise.
6. Preheat the oven to 425°F while the biscuits are in the freezer.
7. Bake the biscuits for 20 minutes, until golden brown. Remove from the oven.
8. Brush the warm biscuits with the remaining melted butter. Serve immediately.
9. Store leftovers, well wrapped, at room temperature for several days. Freeze for longer storage. Biscuits are best when rewarmed before serving.

## Tips from our Bakers

- **Easiest way to cut biscuits:** Pat the dough into a 7" square. Use a sharp knife to divide into nine squares. Nobody said biscuits have to be round!
- **Why cornstarch?** It lowers the protein of the flour, which produces a more tender biscuit.
- **Milk or buttermilk instead of cream?** Of course — you'll need to use a bit more, and the biscuits won't be as tender or rich.
