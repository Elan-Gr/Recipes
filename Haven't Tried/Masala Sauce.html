Masala Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 red onions
  • Some oil
  • 4 tomatoes
  • Ginger and garlic paste
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 green cardamom pods
  • ½ teaspoon cumin
  • 30 grams cashews
  • ½ teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 150 ml water

Instructions

  1. Sweat the red onions in some oil.
  2. Chop up the tomatoes and add to the onions.
  3. Add ginger and garlic paste.
  4. Add the bay leaves, green cardamom pods, cumin, and cashews.
  5. Stew the tomatoes in the sauce for 15–20 minutes.
  6. Add the chili powder, garam masala, and water.
  7. Cook for another 15 minutes.
  8. Blend the sauce.
