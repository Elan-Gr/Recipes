Masala Sauce

  • Sweat 2 red onions in some oil.
  • Chop up 4 tomatoes and add to onions.
  • Add ginger and garlic paste.
  • Add 2 bay leaves, 4 green cardamom pods, ½ teaspoon cumin, 30 grams cashews.
  • Stew tomatoes in sauce for 15–20 minutes.
  • Add ½ teaspoon chili powder, 1 teaspoon garam masala, 150 ml water.
  • Cook for another 15 minutes.
  • Blend sauce.
