Chocolate Icing

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups (255 g) semisweet chocolate chips
  • ½ cup (113 g) half-and-half*

*Substitute ⅓ cup (76 g) cold brewed coffee (or water) for the half-and-half to make a non-dairy icing.

Instructions

  1. In a medium heatproof bowl, combine the chocolate chips and half-and-half.
  2. Heat in the microwave in 30-second increments, stirring between each, until the chocolate melts. (Alternatively, this can be done in a small saucepan over low heat.)
  3. Stir the icing until smooth, then pour/spread over the cake.
  4. Allow the icing to set briefly before slicing and serving.
