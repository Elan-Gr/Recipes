Oven Glazed Smoked Salmon

Ingredients

Brine

  • ¼ cup table salt
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 quarts cold water

Salmon

  • 4 (6- to 8-ounce) skin-on salmon fillets (preferably 1½- to 2-inch thick center-cut)
  • ½ teaspoon vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon minced fresh parsley or chives

Glaze

  • ⅓ cup red wine vinegar (or white wine / champagne vinegar)
  • 3 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 4 teaspoons soy sauce
  • 1½ teaspoons cornstarch
  • ⅛ teaspoon liquid smoke

Instructions

  1. Brine the salmon: Adjust the oven rack to the middle position and heat the oven to 300°F. Dissolve ¼ cup table salt and ¼ cup sugar in 2 quarts of cold water in a large container. Submerge the salmon fillets in the brine and let stand at room temperature for 15 minutes. Remove the fillets and pat them thoroughly dry with paper towels.
  2. Make the glaze: While the salmon brines, combine ⅓ cup red wine vinegar, 3 tablespoons maple syrup, 4 teaspoons soy sauce, 1½ teaspoons cornstarch, and ⅛ teaspoon liquid smoke in a small saucepan, stirring until no lumps remain. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, stirring frequently, and cook until thickened (about 1 minute longer). Remove from heat and transfer 2 tablespoons of the glaze into a small bowl.
  3. Sear and bake: Heat ½ teaspoon vegetable oil in a 12-inch ovensafe nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until just smoking. Place the salmon fillets flesh side down and cook until well browned, 2 to 3 minutes. Flip the fillets and turn the heat down to low. Brush the tops of the fillets with the reserved 2 tablespoons of glaze. Move the skillet to the oven and bake until the centers are still translucent and register 125°F for medium-rare (about 10 to 15 minutes; check after 10 minutes if fillets are under 1 inch thick). Wash and dry your brush.
  4. Glaze and serve: Remove from the oven, brush the remaining glaze over the top and sides of each fillet, and sprinkle with minced fresh parsley or chives before serving.
