Baked English Muffins

Source: King Arthur Baking — <https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/baked-english-muffins-recipe>

These beautiful, high-rising English muffins are baked, not cooked on a griddle. While their interior isn't filled with the signature fissures of a griddle-baked English muffin, their texture is still craggy enough to trap and hold butter and jam.

Prep: 20 mins · Bake: 18 to 20 mins · Total: 1 hr 55 mins · Yield: 12 muffins

Ingredients

  • 2 3/4 cups (330g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
  • 1 teaspoon (6g) table salt
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons (25g) granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons instant yeast
  • 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons (255g) milk, lukewarm
  • 2 tablespoons (28g) butter, melted
  • 2 teaspoons white cider vinegar or yellow vinegar
  • Semolina flour or King Arthur semolina or cornmeal, to coat the muffins

Instructions

  1. Weigh your flour; or measure it by gently spooning it into a cup, then sweeping off any excess.
  2. Stir together all the ingredients except the semolina or cornmeal. Beat for 1 minute at high speed on a stand mixer; the dough will become somewhat smooth.
  3. Scrape the dough into the center of the bowl, cover, and let rise for about 60 minutes, until quite puffy.
  4. Grease a large (18" x 13") baking sheet; or line with parchment. Grease twelve 3 3/4" English muffin rings and place them on the baking sheet. Sprinkle semolina or cornmeal into each ring.
  5. Turn the dough onto a lightly greased or floured work surface. Cut into 12 equal pieces (each a scant 2 ounces, about 54g). Shape into balls. Place each ball into a ring, pressing down to flatten somewhat. Sprinkle with a bit more cornmeal or semolina, and top with a greased baking sheet (or a sheet of parchment, then the baking sheet). The baking sheet should rest atop the rings.
  6. Let the muffins rise for about 60 to 90 minutes, until they've puffed up noticeably. While the dough is rising, preheat the oven to 400°F.
  7. Bake the risen muffins for 10 minutes. Flip the pans over and bake for 5 minutes more. Remove the top pan and bake an additional 3 to 5 minutes, until light golden brown and the interior of one registers about 200°F on an instant-read thermometer.
  8. Remove from the oven and transfer to a rack to cool. Remove the rings as soon as you're able. When completely cool, store muffins in a plastic bag.

Tips from our Bakers

  • No English muffin rings? Shape the dough into well-shaped balls, flatten, and place on the baking sheet without rings. They may be a bit thinner, but will still taste just fine.
