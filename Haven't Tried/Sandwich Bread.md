# Sandwich Bread Recipe

## Ingredients

- 360 g white flour, preferably bread flour (or up to 60% whole wheat)
- 7 g (1 packet) instant yeast
- 113 g water or milk
- 130 g more water
- 50 g oil or melted butter
- 8 g salt
- 25 g sugar

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients in a large bowl until fully combined.
2. Let the dough rest for 2 hours and perform 3 sets of bowl folds:
   - First fold after 30 minutes
   - Second fold after 60 minutes
   - Third fold after 90 minutes
3. At this point, you can put the bread in the fridge for a day or so if you need to make the dough ahead of time. Take it out of the fridge at least 45 minutes before shaping.
4. Shape dough.
5. Proof for 30–60 minutes at room temperature.
6. Bake at 350°F for 30–35 minutes. Cover during baking for best results.

**Yield:** 1 loaf
