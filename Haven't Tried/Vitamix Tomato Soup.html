Vitamix Tomato Soup

From Cucina by Elena — a 20-minute tomato basil soup, light and creamy, that blends and heats entirely in a high-speed blender. Works with canned tomatoes year-round. (The post mentions olive oil in passing, but the recipe card lists none — none included.)

Ingredients

  • 1 (28-ounce) can whole peeled tomatoes (794 g), with their juices
  • ½ cup (120 g) low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth, or water
  • 1 tablespoon (~10 g) coarsely chopped shallot
  • ½ garlic clove (~1.5 g)
  • 5 leaves fresh basil (~2.5 g)
  • ½ tablespoon (~8 g) tomato paste
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • ¼–½ cup (60–120 g) heavy cream, to taste
  • Freshly grated parmigiano cheese, for serving (~10 g)

Instructions

  1. Place the tomatoes (with their juices), broth, shallot, garlic, basil, and tomato paste in a blender. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Blend, gradually increasing to high speed, until completely smooth (1–2 minutes). Vitamix: keep blending until the soup is hot and heavy steam escapes from the lid plug, about 6 minutes (or use the soup setting).
  3. Stir in the heavy cream to taste. Vitamix: blend about 5 seconds to warm through.
  4. Serve hot with freshly grated parmigiano and crusty bread. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.

No Vitamix? No problem

  • Regular blender + stovetop: blend everything smooth (in batches if needed), transfer to a pot, bring to a boil, then simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the cream and return to a simmer.
  • Immersion blender: simmer everything in a pot, then blend right in the pot until silky.
  • No blender at all: finely chop the ingredients, simmer on the stove, then push through a sieve for a smooth texture (peel the tomatoes first if going this route).
  • The friction-heat trick is Vitamix-only — a regular blender won't get the soup hot, and running one on high for 6 minutes isn't a substitute.

Variations

  • Spicy: add chili pepper flakes.
  • Deluxe: caramelized or sautéed onions, sun-dried tomatoes, or extra minced garlic.
  • Dairy-free: cashew cream or non-dairy milk instead of heavy cream (the soup is already creamy without it).
  • Kid-friendly: serve with a grilled cheese for dipping.
  • Fresh tomatoes: steam or boil and peel them, or roast halved at 400°F for 15 minutes with a drizzle of olive oil.

Storage: airtight container, up to 3 days. Reheat in the microwave or on the stovetop, stirring occasionally.

Pairs with: oven baked grilled cheese.
