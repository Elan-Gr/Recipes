# Baked Yeasted Donuts or Breakfast Pastry Dough

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 240 grams (1 cup) whole milk
- 1 packet (7 grams, 2.25 teaspoons) instant yeast
- 62 grams (1/4 cup + 1 teaspoon) sugar
- 85 grams (6 tablespoons) melted unsalted butter
- 1 large egg
- 360 grams (3 cups) all purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Cooking spray

### Donut Glaze

- 120 grams (3 cups) powdered sugar
- 120 grams (1/2 cup) whole milk
- 2 tablespoons melted unsalted butter
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

### For the donuts

1. Mix ingredients with a spoon until mixed.
2. Let dough sit for half an hour at room temperature.
3. Stretch/fold/knead the bread (2–3 minutes).
4. Cover and let sit for another half hour.
5. Stretch/fold/knead the bread (2–3 minutes).
6. Cover and let sit for another half hour.
7. Roll dough into ¼ inch thick rectangle on a lightly floured surface.
8. Cut out donuts.
9. Cover and let rise for 15–20 minutes.
10. Air fry at 350°F or bake at 400°F for 6–8 minutes.

### For the glaze

1. Stretch/fold/knead the bread (2–3 minutes). *(Note: the original lists only this step for the glaze.)*

### Cream cheese Danish filling

Mix 1 lb cream cheese, 4 tablespoons sugar, and vanilla. Mix together, then add whatever jam or filling you want.
