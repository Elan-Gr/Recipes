Dairy-Free Flaky Pie Crust

From Go Dairy Free (Alisa Fleming, Go Dairy Free: The Guide and Cookbook). Uses coconut oil instead of butter — the solid bits of oil set firmly when chilled and create flaky layers as they bake. Works for sweet and savory pies. Refined coconut oil for neutral flavor; virgin coconut oil when a hint of coconut fits (e.g. coconut cream pie, curry pot pie).

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour (180 g)
  • ½ to 1 tablespoon sugar (6–12 g; omit for a savory crust)
  • ½ teaspoon salt (3 g)
  • 6 tablespoons coconut oil, solid or semi-solid (82 g)
  • 3 tablespoons cold water (45 g), plus additional as needed

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, and salt.
  2. Whisk the coconut oil into the dry mixture until coarse crumbs form. You can alternatively use a pastry cutter or fork to cut the coconut oil into the dry mixture.
  3. Add the cold water and stir until it comes together into a dough, bringing it together with your hands when needed. If the dough is too dry, splash in a little more water — in dryer climates you might need up to 6 tablespoons (90 g). The dough should be workable, but not sticky.
  4. Press the dough into a 9-inch pie pan, and crimp the edges as desired.
  5. Fill the pie and bake as directed. If you need a pre-baked crust, use the blind bake option below.

Blind Bake Option

  1. Place a square of parchment paper in the pie crust, lightly pressing it in against the edges and sides. Add pie weights, dried beans, dry rice, or other dry grains until the crust is two-thirds full.
  2. Refrigerate for 30 minutes, then bake 20 minutes at 375°F.
  3. Remove from the oven, lift out the parchment with the weights, and prick the bottom of the crust several times with a fork.
  4. Bake another 15–20 minutes, or until golden.

Yield: one 9-inch pie crust
