Barbqueue Sauce

Ingredients

  • around 1.5 cups (400 grams) ketchup
  • ½ cup (120 grams) apple cider vinegar
  • ¼ cup (50 grams) brown sugar or honey (or less for a less sweet bbq sauce)
  • Spices to taste

Instructions

  1. Mix the ketchup, apple cider vinegar, brown sugar or honey, and spices together.
  2. Heat up on stove or microwave to make sure everything is combined.
