Barbqueue Sauce ✓ tried

From: Barbqueue Sauce.docx

Mix around 1.5 cups (400 grams) ketchup, ½ cup (120 grams) apple cider vinegar, ¼ cup (50 grams) brown sugar or honey (or less for a less sweet bbq sauce), and spices to taste. Mix together and heat up on stove or microwave to make sure everything is combined.
