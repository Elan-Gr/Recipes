Tomato Sauce

Tomato-based sauce. You can customize it by blending with peppers or adding eggplant. Good with pasta, chicken, vegetables.

Ingredients

Makes about 3 cups (1 jar) — enough for 1 lb pasta.

  • 1 medium onion (~150g), diced
  • 3 cloves garlic (~9g), minced
  • 2 tbsp (30ml) olive oil
  • 1 28-oz can (800g) crushed tomatoes

Instructions

  1. Sweat onions and garlic in olive oil.
  2. Add crushed tomatoes.
  3. Let it simmer.
