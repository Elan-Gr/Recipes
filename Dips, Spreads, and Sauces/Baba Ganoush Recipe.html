Baba Ganoush Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs (900 grams) eggplant (2 small-medium eggplants)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 75 grams tahini
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • 20 grams lemon juice (1/2 lemon)
  • 70 grams (1/3 cup) olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Halve eggplants, open side in oil, and roast open side down at 450 degrees for 30–40 minutes.
  2. Scoop out the inside of roasted eggplant, avoiding the skin or the burnt tops.
  3. Mix with tahini, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, salt, parsley.
