Sauces

Technique reference for the four basic sauce families.

White Sauce

Milk/cream-based sauce where you make a roux by mixing 1 tablespoon butter/fat with 1 tablespoon flour/starch, cooking until brown, and then add 1 cup warm milk/half and half. Can customize this by adding up to 150 grams of shredded cheese. Good with pasta, fish, or as a base for creamy soups.

Light Sauce

Stock-based sauce where you make roux by mixing 1 cup fat with 1 tablespoon flour/starch, cook until brown, and then add 1 cup light broth. Can customize by blending with herbs. Good with fish, poultry, vegetables.

Brown Sauce

Stock-based sauce where you make roux by mixing 1 cup fat with 1 tablespoon flour/starch, cook until brown, and then add 1 cup dark broth (broth where bones were roasted). Can customize by blending with cooked vegetables. Good with poultry, steak, vegetables.

Tomato Sauce

Tomato-based sauce where you sweat onions and garlic in olive oil, add crushed tomatoes, and let it simmer. You can customize it by blending with peppers or adding eggplant. Good with pasta, chicken, vegetables.
