Hummus ✓ tried

From: Hummus.docx

Grind chickpeas and water until smooth. Then stir in tahini, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and salt.

  • 1 can (240 grams after draining) chickpeas
  • 20 grams (from ½ a lemon) lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 65–130 grams tahini (1/4–1/2 cup)
  • 30–60 grams water (2–4 tablespoons)
  • 14 grams olive oil (1 tablespoon)
  • Salt to taste
