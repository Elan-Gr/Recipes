# Cane's Sauce

- 110 grams (0.5 cup) mayonnaise
- 60 grams (0.25 cup) ketchup
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ¼ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce

Let sit in fridge for 24 hours.
