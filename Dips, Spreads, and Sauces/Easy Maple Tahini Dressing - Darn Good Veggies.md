# Easy Maple Tahini Dressing

Author: Laurel. Prep Time: 5 minutes. Total Time: 5 minutes. Yield: about ¾ cup. Category: Sides. Method: No cook. Cuisine: American. Diet: Vegan.

A five ingredient wonder that can be used as a dip or sauce too! Perfectly sweet, nutty and creamy, and secretly healthy!

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup tahini (Soom or Trader Joe's brands)
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons pure maple syrup
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- Big pinch salt
- ¼ cup cold water

## Instructions

1. Add all the ingredients except the water to a bowl. Whisk until smooth.
2. Slowly add in the cold water, whisking, until you get the perfect thick, but pourable, consistency.

## Notes

Keep it in the fridge for up to one month!
