# Recipes for Camping Grill and Griddle

**Pizza:** Make New York Pizza Dough, put it on pre-heated griddle, add sliced/shredded cheese, then add sauce and toppings, and bake on grill covered or in oven.

- Smash Burgers
- Pancakes
- French Toast
- Grilled Cheese
- Flatbread
- Eggs/Omelets
- Fried Rice
